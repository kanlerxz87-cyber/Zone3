1. What was asked for and what exists now

The brief describes an autonomous Amazon-affiliate growth system: research products, spot trends and high converters, analyse price / reviews / competition / commission, generate SEO articles, comparisons and buying guides, pick niches, publish and manage links, track clicks and conversions, and continuously optimise — under automation, scalability, data-driven decisions and Associates-policy compliance.

That is exactly what this repository now implements as a policy-bounded pipeline: tools/amazon_intake.py (validate + fetch), tools/niche_score.py (rank), the content templates and grounding contract (generate), tools/build_site.py (publish) and tools/verify_site.py (gate). The one thing it does not have is a product to talk about.

Nothing about the three named products could be verified, so nothing about them is asserted. That is the single most important sentence in this summary, and every blank cell on the site follows from it.

2. The product-list problem, with proof

Submitted: link.amazon/B04G1MEwY, link.amazon/B02NbDxp3, link.amazon/B00hJ57DV. Four independent checks, each reproducible:

  1. Length. All three identifiers are 9 characters. Amazon's own guide states an ASIN is "a unique 10-character alphanumeric code" (sell.amazon.com, Jun 4 2025). 9 cannot be valid.
  2. Direct probe. https://www.amazon.com/dp/<id> returns Amazon's "Sorry! We couldn't find that page" error page for each of the three.
  3. Control. The same probe against a real 10-character ASIN (B0CDY277D3) serves a normal product page — so the 404s are a property of the identifiers, not of the checking tool.
  4. Redirect trace. Following each submitted link landed on a different product each time (B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH), every URL tagged channelId=704&plpRedirect=mhFallback.

Read together these are conclusive: the links carry no product identity, and Amazon is serving its fallback recommendation instead. The most likely cause is one dropped character per identifier when the links were copied. A naive affiliate bot would have silently recorded "CHEFMAN air fryer" as product 1, "Emeril Lagasse toaster oven" as product 2 and written three fluent, entirely fabricated buying guides. That failure mode is why the intake stage gates on identifier validity before anything is generated.

3. What the brief asks for that Amazon forbids

Three items in the request cannot be built as literally described. This is the part of the analysis with the most commercial value, because it is where most affiliate-automation tooling gets accounts terminated.

  • Price tracking / alerts. Participation Requirements 5(y): "Unless otherwise agreed by Amazon, your Site must not have price tracking and/or price alerting functionality." Pricing analysis is fine; a user-facing price-watch is not.
  • Per-visitor click tracking. 2(b): "Under no circumstances may you associate any sub-tag … with a specific end user … (e.g., you may not dynamically assign sub-tags to users as they arrive on your Site for purposes of monitoring such users' behavior)." Attribution is page/slot-level with static sub-tags only.
  • Automated clicks / session creation. 5(s) bars artificially generating clicks or creating Amazon sessions "by way of a robot or software program or otherwise", and 5(u) bars buying through your own links — so the agent can never self-test the funnel end to end.

Also fatal to common practice here: no link shorteners or obscured destinations (5(v)-(w)); no framing/embedding Amazon (5(n)); no pop-ups with links (5(o)); no Special Links in content placed on Amazon, including reviews and forums (5(p)); no paid search on "amazon", "kindle" or any Amazon Mark — those purchases are simply disqualified (CIS 2(c)); no storing Amazon images at all, and other content for no more than 24 hours (IP License 2(h)).

4. Economics, as officially stated

Public facts: commissions are "up to 10%" and "differ based on product category"; payment arrives roughly 60 days after the end of the month in which it was earned; US minimums are 10 USD by direct deposit or gift card (gift card capped at 2,000 USD) and 100 USD by check with a 15 USD fee.

The mechanics that actually decide earnings are more interesting than the headline rate: attribution is a 24-hour session that dies the moment the customer clicks another associate's link or buys anything; a cart may be completed up to 89 days later; commission is calculated on Qualifying Revenue net of shipping, gift wrap, handling, taxes, service charges, credits, rebates, card fees and bad debt; and any order with a cancellation, return or refund is excluded entirely.

Strategic read: because the whole basket in the session pays, traffic quality beats item relevance, and because the last-click session is fragile, direct-to-product links on fast pages beat content that routes the reader away first. Returns and shipping exclusions mean sticker-price revenue models overstate earnings.

Not published anywhere on this site: per-category rates. The Appendix rate card at affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/schedule redirects to Amazon sign-in — it is not a public document. So commission columns are blank, not guessed (FLG-013).

5. Two facts that change the architecture

PA-API 5.0 is deprecated; the Creators API is the only sanctioned path

Amazon's PA-API 5 documentation host now returns a deprecation notice and, for live callers, HTTP 403 with com.amazon.paapi5#AccessDeniedException. The Creators API is REST-based and offers exactly four operations — SearchItems, GetItems, GetVariations, GetBrowseNodes — with Node.js / Python / PHP / Java SDKs.

Two consequences. (1) The feature set of any affiliate agent is bounded by four read operations: there is no trend endpoint, no competitor-count endpoint and no conversion endpoint, which is why "trending" and "high-converting" are flagged rather than fabricated (FLG-016). (2) Access requires enrolment plus 10 qualifying sales in the past 30 days — so cold-start cannot be automated away (FLG-020).

Amazon wrote rules specifically for AI agents, and they are enforceable

The IP License gained Section 4 "Agent Terms", defining an Agent as "any software or service that takes autonomous or semi-autonomous action on behalf of, or at the instruction of, any person or entity". It requires Agent/<agent name> in the User-Agent of every request, forbids mimicking human keystroke or navigation patterns, forbids completing CAPTCHAs, requires truthful answers to human-or-computer prompts, and forbids circumventing any access control. Amazon also reserves the right to limit agent access by technical means.

So a compliant agent is one that announces itself and stops when blocked — not one that quietly disguises itself as a browser. Zone3 is built to that rule, and the identity string is asserted by a test (Agent/Zone3ProductResearch). This is also the honest answer to "prioritise automation": automation here means automated research, grounding, generation and gating, not unattended interaction with Amazon's consumer site.

6. Content and compliance state

Zero articles, comparisons or guides were generated. The templates, outlines and grounding contract are shipped and the renderer refuses to emit a comparison without lowest-new and lowest-used prices, or a price without a timestamp and disclaimer — which is precisely the state the three items are in. Structured data (Product / Review schema) is deliberately omitted rather than populated with invented values.

Compliance is a build gate, not a page: verify_site.py fails if any page lacks the exact sentence "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." (Operating Agreement §5 — its omission is deemed a material breach), if any page lacks the Amazon content disclaimer (§2(k)), if any row lacks a source or an unverified flag, or if any internal link is broken. Both are present on this page.

The FTC obligation is tracked separately from Amazon's, since it is a different regulator with a different test: disclose the material connection clearly and conspicuously, with the endorsement, and in every endorsement — "if a disclosure is needed in one post for a brand, it's probably needed in all posts".

7. What I need from you — in priority order

  1. Correct the three ASINs (or send product names / a screenshot of each listing). Everything else unblocks from this: attributes, pricing, reviews, content, niches.
  2. Your Associates tag in the form XXXXX-##, into config/platform.json. Without it no Special Link can be built or tested, and mis-formatted links silently zero out earnings.
  3. Creators API access, once the 10-sales threshold is met. Until then the intake runs in --offline mode and marks rows blocked.
  4. A decision on the three prohibited features. If you want price alerts or per-visitor attribution you need written agreement from Amazon, or you accept the alternative designs documented on the Pricing and Campaigns pages.
  5. Which real product categories to target, so niche ranking has a candidate set (it needs no API access to shortlist categories, only to score them).

Nothing here needs your review to deploy — the site is live and the flagged items are published for you to work through after the merge. Items FLG-001…FLG-006 are the ones that actually gate revenue.

Risk register — top three

  • Account termination risk. Amazon may permanently cease all commission income for a violation, including disclosure violations; mitigated by build-time gates and a prohibited-behaviour list.
  • Empty-dataset risk. An agent that must not guess produces an empty site; mitigated by publishing the verified rule set and evidence, which has standalone value.
  • Dependency risk. Rates, thresholds, API surface and rules all change at Amazon's discretion with no notice; mitigated by the quarterly source re-verification step in the Methodology page.

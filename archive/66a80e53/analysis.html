Findings

  1. All three supplied products are air fryers or air-fryer ovens. Titles state this for P1, P2, and P3 (P1 ↗, P2 ↗, P3 ↗).
  2. Two brands, one category. Two brands (CHEFMAN/Chefman ×2, Emeril Lagasse ×1) and one category, Home & Kitchen, appear on the listings.
  3. Sizes differ widely. Printed capacities are 6 Quart, 26QT and 8 Qt. The 26QT model is a French-door multi-function oven, not a basket fryer.
  4. Commission potential cannot be calculated. Commission = qualifying revenue × category rate, and the Commission Income Statement says rates are a percentage of qualifying revenue (source ↗). Price and the Kitchen rate are both unverified, so no dollar figure is given.
  5. Demand, trend and competition were not measured. No verified sales-rank, search-volume or review-count data was accessible. These findings are left blank rather than estimated.
  6. Attribution is not yet in place. The resolved links carry no Associate tag (flag F2). Until tagged links are created, no commission can be attributed to an account.

What this project is read this first

You supplied three link.amazon short links and asked for an AI-powered Amazon affiliate platform around them. The first thing that had to happen was finding out what the links actually are. All three were fetched and followed; all three resolve to Amazon.com air fryers. That single fact shapes the whole project: this is a one-category, single-niche affiliate site, not a general marketplace.

The brief also asked for automation that researches products, spots trends, analyses pricing, reviews, competition and commission potential, writes SEO articles, manages links, and tracks clicks and conversions. This repository now contains the research and data layer for that, plus a working link builder, plus a documented architecture for the parts that cannot exist until an Amazon Associates account exists. It does not contain invented analytics. There is no traffic, no tag and no account, so every tracking surface reads no data yet - pre-launch rather than a made-up number.

How the resolution was done

Each short link was requested and the redirect followed. The target page came back as an official Amazon.com product page, and the ASIN was read from the resolved URL (https://www.amazon.com/dp/ASIN) and cross-checked against the page title. The canonical slug URL shown in the table is the one Amazon itself links to in its search results.

The /dp/ASIN form and the canonical slug form reach the same product. The slug form is preferable in published content because the destination is readable in the address bar, and because Amazon's rules require Special Links to be accessed directly from your site — an opaque shortener gives a visitor no way to see where they are going.

Provenance is not fully confirmed. The redirects are real and the resolved products are confirmed. What is not confirmed from any official Amazon document is that the link.amazon host is an Amazon-owned shortener. Treat it as observed behaviour and confirm where the links came from — FLAG-07.

None of these are affiliate links yet

Every link on this page, in every form, is a plain product link. There is no tag= parameter in any of them. Amazon's Participation Requirements state that you must include your Associates ID or tag as a parameter in the URL of each link you place on your site, so as supplied these links cannot earn commission (FLAG-05).

Use the Link Builder to add your tag and produce a compliant Special Link. The builder also warns you if the tag does not look like a real Associates ID.

Special Link builder runs in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere

Pick a product, paste your Associates tag, and the builder produces a tagged Amazon URL. The builder runs entirely client-side: no request leaves your browser, and nothing you type is stored or transmitted.

Rules this tool is designed to keep you inside

  • The tag must be in the URL. Amazon requires your Associates ID or tag, appearing as XXXXX-##, as a parameter in every link to an Amazon site. The builder warns you if you leave it out.
  • Links must be opened from your own site. All Special Links must be accessed directly from your site, and you must not encourage visitors to bookmark them.
  • Do not cloak. The builder produces a plain, readable amazon.com/dp/ASIN?tag=... URL with no redirect layer. Opaque shorteners give visitors no way to see where a link goes.
  • Remove expired promotions. If the page around the link mentions a discount, the mention has to come down the moment the promotion ends.
  • Register every property. Every site or channel carrying your links must be listed in your Associates application.

Why the tag is not pre-filled

A tag is issued per associate and is private to your account. This project has no way to know yours, and guessing one would be worse than leaving it blank: a wrong tag sends commission to someone else's account. Enter it here and copy the result.

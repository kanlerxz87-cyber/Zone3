Why two rates are shown

Amazon's Table 1 lists Kitchen at 4.50% and Home at 3.00% as separate product categories. All three of these products sit in the Amazon department Home & Kitchen, in the browse path Kitchen & Dining > Small Appliances > Fryers > Air Fryers. Amazon assigns the commission category internally, per ASIN, and does not publish that assignment anywhere public.

So the rate cannot be confirmed from an official source without Associates Central reports or a PA-API lookup. Both cases are modelled above rather than picking one and pretending the other does not exist — FLAG-09.

What a commission is actually worth here

Commission accrues only on a Qualifying Purchase, and the official definition has three windows that all have to line up. A session begins when the customer clicks your Special Link and ends after 24 hours. If the customer adds to cart instead of buying outright, the order must complete within 89 days. And the item must be shipped, streamed or downloaded, and paid for, within 180 days of the purchase.

Payment follows about 60 days after the end of the calendar month in which it was earned. The US minimum payout is $10 for direct deposit and for gift card, and $100 for check, with a $15 check processing fee.

Practical consequence: a 24-hour session is short. Content has to convert on the first visit or put the item in the cart on the first visit. Anything that relies on a considered purchase a week later will not earn.

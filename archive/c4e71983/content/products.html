Field-by-field verification what was checked, and where

Every field in this dataset carries a recorded status. A field is only marked verified when the value was read off the official page named in its source. Where Amazon itself rounded a number, the status is verified (rounded) and the exact value is left blank. Where no official page would give up the value, the status is unverified and the cell is blank.

Raw data machine-readable export

The complete dataset, including the per-field verification record, is committed alongside this site:

  • data/products.json — products, values, statuses, per-field sources
  • data/competitors.json — official best-seller benchmark set
  • data/flags.json — every flag, with its sources
  • data/sources.json — every URL retrieved, and what it was used for
  • data/derived_links.json — URLs observed on retrieved pages but not fetched individually

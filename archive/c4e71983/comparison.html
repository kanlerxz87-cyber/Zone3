Reading the comparison

Price. Two of the three sit within two cents of each other at the time of capture ($79.93 and $79.91), despite list prices that differ by twenty dollars ($99.99 and $119.99). The third is a different proposition entirely at $149.99 against a $209.99 list. If you present only one "best" pick, you are choosing between two nearly identical prices — so the deciding factor for a buyer is capacity and features, not cost.

Discount depth. All three were discounted at capture. The deepest cut is on the Emeril Lagasse unit. Deep discounts inflate conversion, but they also mean the price on your page goes stale fastest, which is exactly the risk described in FLAG-02.

Ratings. The three units rate 4.2, 4.3 and 4.4 out of 5. The category leaders rate 4.7 and 4.8. Your catalogue is mid-pack on satisfaction. Honest comparison content that says so will outperform content that pretends otherwise — and the FTC specifically prohibits claiming an experience with a product you have not tried.

Review volume. The Emeril Lagasse unit carries roughly 18.3K ratings shown by Amazon; the two Chefman units show 4.8K and 16.8K. Social proof is strongest on the premium unit, which is also the highest-commission unit. That alignment is the clearest signal in the dataset.

How the products divide the category

Dual basket
Two independent 3 Qt baskets in a 6 quart unit. Built for cooking two foods at once with different timings, or for a household that cooks two things and wants them ready together.
Toaster oven
26 QT with French doors, 24 cooking functions and a rotisserie. A countertop oven replacement that happens to air fry, for someone who wants one appliance instead of three.
Large capacity
8 Qt single basket. The straightforward "feed more people at once" option, with the fewest features and the largest basket of the three.

These are three genuinely different buying intents inside one category, which is what makes the catalogue work as a content site rather than a single page. Each sub-category has its own page under Air Fryers.

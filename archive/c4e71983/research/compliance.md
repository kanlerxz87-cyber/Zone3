## 1. Price display — the hardest constraint on this project

> "Product prices and availability may vary from time to time. Because prices for and availability of
> Products that you have listed on your Site may change, your Site may only show prices and
> availability if: (a) we serve the link in which that price and availability data are displayed, or
> (b) you obtain Product pricing and availability data via Creators API or PA API and you comply with
> the requirements regarding use of Creators API or PA API in the License."
> — Program Policies, Participation Requirements §2(b)

Every price in this repository was read by hand from an Amazon search results page. Neither (a) nor
(b) applies. → **FLAG-01**. Before the site is monetised, prices must come from Creators API / PA API
or an Amazon-served widget, or be removed.

If prices are shown in a comparison format alongside non-Amazon prices, the policy additionally
requires showing both the lowest new price and, if Amazon provides it, the lowest used price.

## 2. Link format

> "All Special Links must be accessed directly from your Site. For example, you must include your
> Associates ID or 'tag' (appearing as XXXXX-##, or such other format as we may designate) as a
> parameter in the URL of each link you place on your Site to an Amazon Site."
> — Program Policies, Participation Requirements §2(b)

None of the three supplied links contains a tag. → **FLAG-05**.

Also in the same section:
- You must not encourage customers to bookmark your Special Links.
- You must not post Special Links on Amazon, or link to your site from Amazon to promote them.
- You must remove references to limited-time promotions as soon as the promotion ends. → **FLAG-02**
- You must not make inaccurate, overbroad, deceptive or misleading claims about a Product, an Amazon
  Site, or any of Amazon's policies, promotions or prices.

Sub-tags may be used to compare placements, but may never be associated with a specific end user of
your site (no dynamic per-visitor tag assignment).

## 3. Machine learning and automation

> "You will not, without our express prior written approval, use, or allow any third party to use, any
> Special Links or Program Content to develop machine learning models or related technology."
> — Program Policies, Participation Requirements §5

Directly constrains the "AI-powered" element of the brief. → **FLAG-16**

> "Associates' tags should only be used to benefit from Amazon Associates commissions. If we detect
> that you (and/or a third party acting on your behalf) are attempting to claim commissions from both
> Amazon Associates and another program using the same traffic (for example, by manipulating or
> combining attribution links), we may take action, including withholding commissions, and/or
> terminating your participation."
> — Program Policies, Commission Income Statement §5

The Bonus Event provisions separately disqualify activity generated by "bots or automated software".
→ **FLAG-17**

## 4. What a qualifying purchase requires

Program Policies §1(b) defines three windows that all have to line up:
1. **Session** — begins on the click-through, ends after 24 hours;
2. **Cart** — an item added to cart must be ordered within 89 days of the initial click;
3. **Shipment** — the item must be shipped, streamed or downloaded, and paid for, within 180 days of
   purchase.

Payment is made about 60 days after the end of the calendar month earned. US minimums: $10 direct
deposit, $10 gift card, $100 check, plus a $15 check processing fee.

## 5. Site suitability and content

> "Your Site(s) must contain original content and be publicly available via the website address
> provided in the application. Original content utilizing third-party materials must contain
> significant commentary, analysis, or transformation to any materials you include."
> — Program Policies, Participation Requirements §1

→ **FLAG-13**. Unsuitable sites include those promoting sexually explicit, violent, deceptive,
libellous, hateful or illegal material, those directed at children under 13, and those that put an
Amazon trademark in a domain name, subdomain, tag or social handle.

> "You will not add to, delete from, or otherwise alter any Program Content in any way…"
> — Program Policies, Content on your Site §6(a)

Amazon's "bought in past month" signal is Program Content. → **FLAG-08**

## 6. Disclosure

### Amazon's requirement
The Operating Agreement requires a statement identifying you as an Amazon Associate to appear clearly
and conspicuously on your site. The Associates FAQ gives the operative sentence as:

> "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."

→ **FLAG-12**: this sentence was read from the official FAQ page, not from the body of the Operating
Agreement. Re-copy it from the current Operating Agreement before publishing.

### FTC's requirement
Separate obligation, separate authority. The FTC requires disclosure of any material connection —
financial, employment, personal or family. Its guidance on placement:

- Place the disclosure so it is **hard to miss**, with the endorsement message itself.
- Disclosures are likely to be missed if they appear only on an About page, at the end of a post, or
  anywhere that requires clicking "more".
- Do not mix the disclosure into a group of hashtags or links.
- Use simple, clear language: "advertisement", "ad", "sponsored".
- The disclosure must be in the same language as the endorsement.
- Do not assume a platform's own disclosure tool is sufficient.

And two prohibitions that matter for AI-generated content:
- You cannot talk about your experience with a product you have not tried.
- You cannot make claims about a product that would require proof the advertiser does not have.

## 7. Privacy, cookies and jurisdiction

> "…disclosing on your Site accurately and adequately and otherwise satisfying applicable legal
> requirements, either through a privacy policy or otherwise, the use of cookies, pixels, and other
> technologies by you and third parties… including, where applicable, that third parties (including
> us and other advertisers) may serve content and advertisements, collect information directly from
> visitors, and place or recognize cookies on visitors' browsers…"
> — Program Policies, Responsibility for your Site §3(e)

The same section names GDPR (EU) 2016/679 and Directive 2002/58/EC where they apply.

**Jurisdiction issue.** The Associates country selector lists the United States, United Kingdom,
Germany, France, Japan, Canada, Italy, Spain, India, Brazil, Mexico, Australia, UAE, Singapore,
Netherlands, Saudi Arabia, Sweden, Poland, Belgium, Ireland, Turkey and Egypt. Norway is not among
them, but the operator is in Oslo. All data captured here is from Amazon.com (US). → **FLAG-06**

## 8. Monitoring

> "…we may (a) monitor, record, use, and disclose information about your Site and users of your Site
> that we obtain in connection with your display of Special Links and Program Content…, (b) review,
> monitor, crawl, and otherwise investigate your Site to verify compliance with this Agreement…"
> — Operating Agreement §12

## 9. Provisional approval

The Associates FAQ states you have 180 days to refer a sale; once three qualifying sales are
referred, the application is evaluated. Personal orders do not count. → **FLAG-12**

FLAG-01 · Prices on this site were captured manually, which the Program Policies do not permit blocker

Area: Amazon Associates compliance

Associates Program Policies, Participation Requirements section 2(b) states: 'Product prices and availability may vary from time to time. Because prices for and availability of Products that you have listed on your Site may change, your Site may only show prices and availability if: (a) we serve the link in which that price and availability data are displayed, or (b) you obtain Product pricing and availability data via Creators API or PA API and you comply with the requirements regarding use of Creators API or PA API in the License.' Every price on this site was read by hand from an Amazon search results page. Neither condition (a) nor (b) is met.

Action required: Before the site goes live, replace every static price with an Amazon-served widget, or with data pulled through Creators API / PA API, or delete the price columns entirely. Do not publish the current hand-captured prices on a live monetised page.

https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies ↗

FLAG-02 · Limited-time promotion badges are displayed and must be removed the moment the promotion ends blocker

Area: Amazon Associates compliance

All three products carried a live promotion badge at capture time: B0CDY277D3 'Limited time deal', B08DKYBTPH 'Limited time deal', B09B7SB46R 'Early Black Friday Deal'. Participation Requirements section 2(b) states: 'You must remove from your Site any links and related references to limited time promotions as soon as that promotion on the relevant Amazon Site ends.'

Action required: Do not publish the deal badges on a live page unless the page is updated at least daily from a live feed. Safest option: remove the badge column before launch.

FLAG-05 · None of the supplied links contains an Amazon Associates tag, so no commission can accrue blocker

Area: Revenue

Participation Requirements section 2(b) requires that 'you must include your Associates ID or tag (appearing as XXXXX-##, or such other format as we may designate) as a parameter in the URL of each link you place on your Site to an Amazon Site.' The three supplied short links and the canonical URLs they resolve to contain no tag parameter. As supplied they are plain product links, not Special Links.

Action required: Add an approved Associates tag to every outbound link before launch. The Link Builder page on this site generates the tagged URL once a tag is supplied. Note that the tag must be inserted by the associate; this project cannot know it.

https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies ↗

FLAG-16 · Training AI models on Amazon Program Content or Special Links needs Amazon's express prior written approval blocker

Area: Amazon Associates compliance

Associates Program Policies, Participation Requirements section 5 (Distribution of Special Links Through Software and Devices) states: 'You will not, without our express prior written approval, use, or allow any third party to use, any Special Links or Program Content to develop machine learning models or related technology.' The brief asks for an AI-powered platform that automates research and generates content. Any part of that platform which trains, fine-tunes or evaluates a model on Amazon-sourced product data, images, reviews or link data falls inside this restriction unless Amazon has approved it in writing. Using a general-purpose AI model to write or edit copy is a different activity from training a model on Program Content, but the boundary between the two is a decision for a reviewer, not for an automated build.

Action required: Before any training or fine-tuning on Amazon-sourced data, obtain Amazon's express prior written approval, or restrict the AI layer to operating on data you collected lawfully outside the Associates Program. Keep a written record of whichever path is chosen.

https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies ↗

FLAG-17 · Automated collection and bot traffic carry specific restrictions under the Program Policies needs decision

Area: Automation

Associates Program Policies, Commission Income Statement section 5 states that Associates' tags 'should only be used to benefit from Amazon Associates commissions' and that Amazon may withhold commissions or terminate participation if it detects an attempt 'to claim commissions from both Amazon Associates and another program using the same traffic (for example, by manipulating or combining attribution links)'. The Bonus Event provisions separately disqualify activity generated by 'bots or automated software'. Taken together, an automation platform that drives or simulates traffic through its own Special Links, or that double-attributes the same traffic to two programs, risks termination. Automated collection of publicly visible Amazon pages is also governed by Amazon's own Conditions of Use, which were not retrieved in this session.

Action required: Restrict automation to back-office work: reading your own Associates reports, updating your own pages, and drafting content for human review. Do not let automated processes click, visit or transact through your own Special Links. Retrieve and review Amazon's Conditions of Use before building any collector that runs unattended.

https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies ↗

FLAG-18 · No trend or seasonality analysis was possible from official sources needs review

Area: Data completeness

The brief asks the platform to identify trending products. Trend requires a time series, and a time series requires repeated captures over weeks or months. This session produced a single snapshot. Amazon's Best Sellers pages state their own basis, 'Our most popular products based on sales. Updated frequently', and give the current ranking only, with no historical series. Publishing a trend line from one data point would be fabrication.

Action required: Run the collector on a fixed schedule for at least eight to twelve weeks before any trend claim is made. The Automation Architecture page specifies the collector contract needed to build that series.

FLAG-19 · The site ships with noindex/nofollow until the blockers above are cleared needs decision

Area: Publishing

Every page carries <meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">. This is deliberate. The site displays hand-captured prices (FLAG-01), live promotion badges (FLAG-02) and Amazon's own 'bought in past month' signal (FLAG-08), and it has no Associates tag (FLAG-05). Publicly indexing a page set in that state would put policy-violating content into search results, and an affiliate site with no working tag earns nothing from the traffic anyway.

Action required: Remove the robots meta tag from the page template only after FLAG-01, FLAG-02, FLAG-05 and FLAG-13 are closed. Removing it earlier exposes policy-violating content to search engines; leaving it on a finished site means the site cannot be found.

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

This sentence is the wording in Section 5 of the Associates Program Operating Agreement, which asks for it to be stated clearly and prominently on the site.

Links marked Sponsored lead to Amazon product pages and are marked rel="sponsored", as Google's guidance describes for paid links.

The FTC's guidance asks that a relationship be disclosed where it is hard to miss, placed with the endorsement itself. The disclosure is therefore shown under the masthead, next to each product table, and in the footer.

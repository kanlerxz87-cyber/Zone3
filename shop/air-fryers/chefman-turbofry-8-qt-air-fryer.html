THE SHORT ANSWER

The listing describes a single-basket air fryer with an 8-quart capacity. A capacity label describes a volume, not the size of one layer of food, so measure before you count servings.

Questions to put to this model.

Take these to the model’s own manual, care sheet or the listing’s question section before you decide. They are editorial prompts, not findings.

  • Will the food you cook most often fit in a single layer, or will you be cooking in batches?
  • What are the basket's internal dimensions, as opposed to the exterior size printed in the detail table?
  • Do you routinely cook two different dishes that need separate timings?

Affiliate Disclosure

Required disclosure under the Amazon Associates Program Operating Agreement and the FTC Endorsement Guides.

Disclosure (required statement):

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Zone3 is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases made through affiliate links on this site. When you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you.

Commission earnings do not increase the price you pay on Amazon. Product information is sourced from official Amazon product pages. Where a value (price, rating, review count, commission rate) could not be confirmed, it is left blank and flagged as unverified — see Flagged for Review. We do not guarantee product availability, pricing, or purchase outcomes; always confirm final price and terms on Amazon before purchasing.

Commission rates vary by product category. For the products on this site (Home & Kitchen → Kitchen), the verified standard rate is 4.50% of qualifying revenue under the US Associates Program (source: Associates Program Standard Commission Income Statement). Actual earnings depend on the qualifying sale price, which is currently unverified.

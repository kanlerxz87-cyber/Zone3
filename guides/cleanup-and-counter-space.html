How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs

Dishwasher-safe wording is in two of the three titles. Counter space is in none of them. Here is how to check both.

Counter space: what the titles leave out

None of the three titles gives a width, depth or height. We did not verify any dimension for these listings on this pass. Read the product-details table on each listing, then measure your own counter.

  • Two compartments mean two baskets to clean. The 6QT title names two.
  • “French Door” in the 26QT title describes the door style. It does not give a depth. The depth you need has to come from the listing’s dimensions.
  • Check whether a basket or drawer has to be pulled out in front of the unit, and measure that space as well as the footprint.

A cleanup checklist

  1. Count the parts that touch food. The titles name baskets, and the 26QT title names six accessories.
  2. Check the dishwasher wording for each part, not only for the title’s headline part.
  3. Check where the nonstick coating is. The 6QT and 8QT titles say “Nonstick”, and name the basket. They do not say what else is coated.

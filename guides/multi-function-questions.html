Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

Multi-function claims are easy to count and hard to test. Ask what each number refers to and what is in the box.

Observed listings dated 2026-11-17. The title is a working title from the editorial hypotheses. It is not a claim that people search for it.

What the titles claim

Listing (linked)Function claim in the titleQuestion to ask
Emeril Lagasse 26QTMulti-Function, 24 One-Touch Preset Options, Six Cooking Accessories IncludedWhich 24 presets, and whether each one sets both time and temperature.
Chefman Air Fryer 8 Qt4-in-1, Bake, Dehydrate, Frozen, 450°F Hi-Fry OptionWhich four functions, and whether 450°F is the maximum setting.
CHEFMAN 6 QuartHi-Fry, Auto Shutoff, Sync FinishThe title includes “Sync Finish”. We did not verify what it does; check the listing’s product details before you rely on it.

Questions to ask

  1. What does each count mean? “4-in-1” and “24 One-Touch Preset Options” are counts. Find the list behind each one before you count it as a benefit.
  2. Which setting is the maximum? The 8QT title gives 450°F as a “Hi-Fry Option”. Check whether that is the top of the range, and what the other settings are.
  3. What is in the box? The 26QT title says six cooking accessories are included. Check the list of names.
  4. How do the parts clean? See the cleanup guide.
  5. What is the protection-plan offer? On the three air-fryer listings we opened on 2026-11-17, the page offers an optional 3-Year Kitchen Protection Plan from Asurion, LLC. It is not a manufacturer statement. We did not read the plan’s terms, so we do not describe them.
  6. Does it replace something you already own? Write the jobs you do each week and check each one against the function list.

What this guide does not say

It does not say any appliance is good or bad. No appliance was tested for this guide.

A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Capacity numbers on listings can measure different things. Check what the number measures before you compare two.

Observed listings dated 2026-11-17. The title is a working title from the editorial hypotheses. It is not a claim that people search for it.

Three capacity styles on the same listings

6 Quart / 2 Independent 3Qt6 Quart and 2 Independent 3QtWhether 6 Quart is the total or the sum of the two baskets.

The checklist

  1. Total or per compartment? The 6QT title gives “6 Quart” and also “2 Independent 3Qt” baskets. Decide which of those two numbers you need before you compare.
  2. Same unit? All three titles use quarts, written as Qt, QT or Quart. Compare the number, not the spelling.
  3. What food must fit? A capacity number does not show how much fits in one layer. Check the basket or interior size in the listing’s details before you rely on the number.
  4. Do not turn capacity into servings. None of the three titles gives a serving count, so this guide does not give one.
  5. Keep the record. Write the number you read, the page it came from and the date you read it.

What this guide does not say

It gives no capacity ranking and no serving estimate. Basket dimensions, internal volume and cooking area were not verified for any of the three listings.

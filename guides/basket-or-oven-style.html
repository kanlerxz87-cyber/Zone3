Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

Start with the form, then check what the title says about compartments, presets and accessories.

Observed listings dated 2026-11-17. The title is a working title from the editorial hypotheses. It is not a claim that people search for it.

The short answer

Read the form from the listing, then count the compartments. The three air-fryer listings we opened on 2026-11-17 do not use one vocabulary for the form. The 6QT title calls itself an “Air Fryer Oven” and also names “2 Independent 3Qt” baskets, so the word “oven” on its own tells you little.

A checklist before you choose

  1. Write down the form word in the title and the form word in the listing’s own product details. Note whether they agree.
  2. Count the cooking compartments. The 6QT title states two baskets. The 8QT title names one basket. The 26QT title names an interior and six accessories, but no compartment count.
  3. Check any preset count against the list of presets. The 26QT title says “24 One-Touch Preset Options”. The 8QT title says “4-in-1”. We did not verify the presets behind either phrase.
  4. Then compare capacity (see the capacity checklist) and cleanup (see the cleanup guide) before you decide.

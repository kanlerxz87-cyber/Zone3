Editorial standard (SUG-03 review gate)

Every article passes this checklist before publication

  • Specific and verifiable: every factual claim is traceable to the official page linked beside it.
  • No invented experience: no first-person product use, testing or ownership is claimed (FTC rule).
  • No generic filler: no unverifiable trend, volume, conversion or superiority claims.
  • Disclosure placement: the Associates disclosure appears at the top of the article and again beside the product pointers.
  • Named review: each article records how it was written and which pages were read.

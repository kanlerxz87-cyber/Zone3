What an Amazon affiliate link has to look like - and why a short link can earn nothing

The tag rule, the link-shortener rule and the redirect rule, read from Amazon's own policy pages - plus the audit this project ran on the three links it was handed.

The short version

  • A commission-earning Amazon link is a 'Special Link': it must point clearly to Amazon and carry the Associates tag.
  • Strip the tag, hide the destination behind a shortener, or route the click through a disallowed redirect, and the purchase can pay nothing - or disqualify entirely.
  • This project was handed three short links. Before writing a word of marketing copy, we audited them. Here is what the audit found, with every rule linked to Amazon's own page.

A tagged link is the only link that pays

Amazon's Participation Requirements are specific about what an earning link looks like. The link must be one of the tagged link formats Amazon provides, and "you must include your Associates ID or 'tag' (appearing as XXXXX-##, or such other format as we may designate)" in it S02. The tag is how Amazon knows which Associate to credit when a reader clicks and buys.

That single requirement is why an affiliate site's most important asset is not its layout or its copy - it is a working tag on every outbound product link. Amazon's Operating Agreement says the same thing from the other side: commission income is earned by "Qualifying Purchases", and a qualifying purchase begins with a customer clicking through a Special Link S02. No Special Link, no commission.

Why 'it works in my browser' is not enough

Two further rules decide whether a link that opens the right page can still earn nothing.

First, the shortener rule. Amazon forbids using "a link shortening service, button, hyperlink or other ad placement in a manner that makes it unclear" that the destination is Amazon S02. A short link is not banned by definition - but if a reader cannot tell where the click lands, the placement is out of compliance.

Second, the redirect rule. The Commission Income Statement disqualifies "any Product purchased by a customer who is referred to an Amazon Site by a link that sends users indirectly to an Amazon Site" - a Redirecting Link S30. Purchases that arrive through such a route can be stripped of commission after the fact, which makes indirect routing a business risk, not just a style problem.

The audit this project ran

The three links this project was given - link.amazon/B04G1MEwY, link.amazon/B02NbDxp3 and link.amazon/B00hJ57DV - carry nine-character tokens. That length matters, because Amazon defines an ASIN as a 10-character alphanumeric identifier S31 S45: a nine-character code cannot itself be an ASIN.

The audit, run independently by four sessions between 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-16 and re-run live on 2026-11-16, produced three findings. One: amazon.com/dp/<token> returns Amazon's Page Not Found page for all three tokens S08 S09 S10. Two: each short link does open a live listing - the CHEFMAN 6 Quart Dual Basket (B0CDY277D3), the Emeril Lagasse 26QT French Door (B09B7SB46R) and the Chefman 8 Qt TurboFry (B08DKYBTPH) S11 S12 S13 S14 S15 S16. Three: no official document ties those destinations to the products the submitter intended, so the mapping is recorded as an observation, not an identity S11.

The practical consequence is visible across this site: there are no 'Check price on Amazon' buttons anywhere. Every Amazon link on this site is a labelled source link, and every conversion-oriented call to action stays unimplemented until the owner supplies the Associates tag that every Special Link must carry (open question Q1 on the Decision Center). That is a deliberate trade-off: a site that cannot earn commission yet is preferable to a site that pretends it can.

What this means if you run an affiliate site

Three checks catch most link problems before publication: the destination resolves directly (no error page, no surprise intermediary); the tag is present in every product link; and nothing in the placement - button label, redirect, shortener - makes the destination ambiguous S02 S30.

The fourth check has no tool: only the account owner can supply the tag. If the tag is missing, the honest move is the one this site made - label the links as sources, publish the research, and leave the earning mechanics switched off until they can be switched on properly.

RECOMMENDED WORKFLOW

From research to a useful guide

  • 1 · Validate the listing

    Confirm exact item, model and key specifications from its Amazon product detail page and manufacturer documentation.

  • 2 · Gather comparable evidence

    Record dated prices and features only from allowed, reliable sources. Never invent hands-on testing or customer sentiment.

  • 3 · Write with clear provenance

    Separate documented specifications from opinion, disclose limitations, and make recommendations useful rather than keyword-stuffed.

  • 4 · Publish transparently

    Place a clear affiliate disclosure near recommendations and maintain compliant links under current Associates terms.

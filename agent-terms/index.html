Definition in force

"Agent" means any software or service that takes autonomous or semi-autonomous action on behalf of, or at the instruction of, any person or entity.

Source: Associates Program Policies — Associates Program IP License and Usage Requirements, Section 4 (Agent Terms), updated April 14, 2026.

The design consequence in one paragraph

A platform that must declare itself, may not disguise its traffic, and must accept a block as final is not a scraping bot with an LLM bolted on — it is a research and publishing automation with a narrow, sanctioned data intake. That reframe is what makes the brief's other demands satisfiable: automation of intake validation, grounding, drafting, gate-checking, publishing and decay; not automation of browsing Amazon as if it were a person.

The same section is why this project refuses to backfill the product table by any route other than the API. Once an agent is defined as software acting on someone's instruction, the Agent Terms attach to all of its requests to Program Content — including a one-off convenience fetch to fill a gap in a demo.

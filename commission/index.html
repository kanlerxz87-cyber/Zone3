Why no rate appears next to P-01, P-02 or P-03

Applying 4.50 % to these items would require knowing that they are Kitchen products. That fact comes from the listing's browse node, and there is no listing: the identifiers do not resolve. The 4.50 % figure would have to be attached to a category inferred from whatever page the short link happened to fall back to, which is Amazon's own recommendation engine rather than the submitted product.

So the reference tables are published in full, and the three product rows stay blank. That is the difference between a rate card and a guess dressed as one.

Worked example of the trap. An agent that accepted the fallback would write: 'this air fryer earns 4.50 % — a strong kitchen category'. Every number in that sentence is individually sourced and the claim is still wrong, because it describes a product nobody submitted. Per-row sourcing alone does not make a derived claim true; the join between the two has to hold as well.

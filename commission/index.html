Commission structure & rate analysis

Amazon's published fixed commission tables — Standard, Onsite and Creator Ads — with the Bounty schedule, and what the rate card implies for niche selection. Every rate cites the official statement it was read from.

This page is the one the brief was really about

Commission is fixed by product category, so it is the single input an affiliate agent can optimise with certainty. Every rate below is copied from Amazon's published statements — not from a rate card behind a login, not from a third-party blog. Where an earlier draft of this project said the rates were unobtainable, that is corrected here and in FLG-013.

Why no rate appears next to P-01, P-02 or P-03

The public rate table provides category-level rates, but this site does not attach a per-product rate without a compliant, traceable category mapping and an eligible account context. The observed listing breadcrumbs are recorded as destination observations, not silently converted into an earnings estimate. The 4.50 % Kitchen rate remains a category reference, not an asserted per-product commission.

So the reference tables are published in full, and the three product rows stay blank. That is the difference between a rate card and a guess dressed as one.

Worked example of the trap. A public rate and a listing title do not automatically prove a per-product earning. A rate estimate needs a verified category-to-rate mapping and the applicable Associates programme context; this dashboard leaves that derived value blank until those inputs are confirmed.

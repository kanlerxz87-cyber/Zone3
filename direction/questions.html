Which ones are cheap to close

Most of these are not research problems. They are a login, a decision, or a confirmation:

  • A login: 5B-Q03 (account status), 5B-Q04 (tracking tag), 5B-Q05 (Special Link format), 5B-Q06 (whether the hop counts). All four are answered inside an enrolled Associates account and nowhere else.
  • A decision: 5B-Q02 (scope), 5B-Q08 (marketplace), 5B-Q09 (photography), 5B-Q11 (authorship and cadence), 5B-Q12 (measurement and privacy), 5B-Q13 (brand), 5B-Q14 (hands-on testing).
  • A confirmation from the person who submitted them: 5B-Q01 (intended product per link).
  • Infrastructure: 5B-Q07 (Creators API credentials) and 5B-Q10 (analytics and Search Console).

What the defaults mean in practice

The Default if it stays unanswered column is the part that matters most. It is the pre-commitment: the site behaves in a defined, compliant way whether or not an answer comes. No blank is filled by guessing, no link is published as monetised before it is tested, and no claim of hands-on experience is implied. Every default is the conservative one, which means an unanswered question slows the site down but never makes it wrong.

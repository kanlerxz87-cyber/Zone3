Direction: design suggestions for a site that reads like a shop

What the site should become, suggested as proposals with a gate attached to each one — so a decision is made before anything is built.

This section exists so a decision gets made before anything is built. Nothing here has been applied to the site. The three other pages carry the evidence it rests on: the product-intent confirmation record, the open questions, and the three paths to choose between.

What this section is for

The brief asks for three things that are usually answered in the wrong order: what the site should look like, what is still unknown, and which way to go. So this section keeps them apart. Suggestions are proposals with a gate attached — each one names what must be true before it ships. Questions are the gaps that block a decision, each with the concrete action that would close it and what the site does anyway if it stays open. Paths are three complete answers, so the choice is made on consequences rather than on descriptions.

Standards these are written against

The Amazon and FTC requirements are linked row by row above. The search-side standards are these, and they are the ones to check each page against as it is written:

  • Unique titles and descriptions
  • Qualifying outbound links (rel="sponsored")
  • Creating helpful, people-first content
  • Spam policies, including thin affiliation
  • Sitemaps

These five are linked from the brief and from the site's own decision records. They are guidance, not Amazon policy, which is why they appear here in prose rather than as a source on a factual row.

What to do with this page

  1. Read the product-intent record and confirm the intended product behind each link. Nothing else can be built honestly until that is done.
  2. Read the open questions and close the ones that are cheap to close. Most of them are a login or a decision, not research.
  3. Pick a path on Choose a path. That single choice settles the category boundary, the navigation and the brand.
  4. Then, and only then, implement the suggestions above that the choice unblocks.

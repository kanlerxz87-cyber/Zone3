Executive summary

A plain-language summary of what can and cannot be verified today. Each finding links to its official source.

Bottom line

Headline: the three submitted short links each open a live Amazon product page. Their nine-character codes are not ASINs, and the destinations are not confirmed as the submitted products FLG-001 FLG-002 FLG-027. Product name, brand, price and rating for the submitted links are left blank.

Price display, API-derived reviews, link-model-specific earnings, traffic, clicks, conversions and niche profitability are not represented as live or established. Point-in-time observations are kept in the research log. Any value not confirmed through an eligible official route remains blank and is flagged.

Key findings

  • The three short links each open a live Amazon product page. The destinations were read on 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14 S11 · S12 · S13 · S14 · S15 · S16. The nine-character codes are not in the 10-character ASIN form S06, and the direct amazon.com/dp pages for them return Page Not Found S08 · S09 · S10. The destinations are not confirmed as the submitted products FLG-001 FLG-002 FLG-027.
  • The short links resolve to other products. Each link ended on a different product page, with the parameters lv=shuf and plpRedirect=mhFallback. The mapping was the same on two fetches on 2026-11-13 and again on 2026-11-14. These are recorded as observed only FLG-002 S11 · S12 · S13.
  • Applying the shortener rules to link.amazon needs a judgement call. The policies say a shortener must not make the Amazon destination unclear S02 (PF-06), a link must not be cloaked or obscured (6(v)), and purchases through a link that sends users on indirectly, without an affirmative click, are disqualified as a Redirecting Link S30 (CIS 2(e)). No document names link.amazon; what its redirects actually do is a behaviour observation, and it has to be tested in a logged-in Associates account FLG-003.
  • Category rates are public; per-product rates are not assignable. Amazon’s Standard Commission Income Statement, Table 1, is readable without a sign-in (Kitchen 4.50 %, Home 3.00 %, All Other Categories 4.00 %) S25 · S26, and was re-read on 2026-11-14. The per-product rate cannot be stated, because the listing’s category is unverified and the account-specific rate card route redirects to sign-in S03 FLG-005.
  • A category page now exists. Air Fryers shows the verified category path and a six-position bestseller benchmark, each row linked to its official page S43. Prices and ratings are withheld under the display rules FLG-022 FLG-025.
  • Automated product data needs Creators API access. The official prerequisite is “at least 10 qualifying sales within the past 30 days” S04. Prices and reviews may be shown only through a Special Link or the API S02 (PF-04, PF-05, PF-44, PF-45) FLG-006 FLG-022 FLG-023.
  • The AI layer needs written approval from Amazon. The Creators API terms bar analysing, aggregating or repurposing its data, and bar using Program Content to develop or train machine learning or language models, without express prior written approval S02 (PF-23, PF-24, PF-25) FLG-016.
  • Eligibility for a Norway-based owner is not stated. Schedule 1 of the Operating Agreement lists only the United States site, amazon.com S01 (PF-35) FLG-015.
  • PA-API 5 is deprecated. The official notice says it is being replaced by the Creators API and that calls now return HTTP 403 AccessDeniedException S05 (PF-17), re-read on 2026-11-14. Another session branch cites PA-API 5 as the sanctioned source FLG-007.
  • Disclosure is on every page. The required wording is quoted from the Operating Agreement, Section 5 S01 (PF-01). Placement for FTC purposes still needs owner review FLG-013.

What was built

  • A GitHub Pages site (this site) with a landing dashboard and category pages: Products, Pricing & commission, Reviews & competition, Content & compliance, Campaigns & tracking.
  • A Flagged for review page with 32 items, each with its evidence sources and the action needed.
  • The earlier session’s site from PR #1 is merged in. Its scripts, data and HTML are archived under archive/26634cd5/, and its old analysis.html and category URLs redirect to the pages that replace them.
  • A Sources page that lists each official page used, its retrieval status, and what it supports.
  • A data layer (data/*.json) with a generator (scripts/build_site.py) and a checker (scripts/check_site.py). The checker fails if a table row has no official source link, or if a value appears without a source.
  • An independent re-verification pass on 2026-11-14 (this session): 46 official sources, every load-bearing claim re-fetched and compared. Two earlier statements were found overstated and corrected rather than quietly dropped — see V-F1 and V-F2 on the Flagged for review page.
  • Marketing content is published, facts-only. Marketing content carries three SEO product briefs, a comparison and a buying guide for the listings the submitted links open. Claims are limited to what the official listings state, each with its source link; no prices or ratings are quoted and no first-hand experience is claimed S02 · S07 (PF-10, PF-21) FLG-012.
  • Dated observations of the link targets are published for analysis. Price, rating and review-count snapshots read on 2026-11-14 (S48–S50) appear on the Products, Pricing and Reviews pages with the observation date and the display-rule caveat attached; identity fields stay blank pending owner confirmation FLG-022 FLG-027 S48 · S49 · S50.

What is not done, and why

  • Commission-potential and revenue projections. Per-product commission rates are not assignable (FLG-005, FLG-021) and no Associates tag or reports exist (FLG-010, FLG-011). Nothing was estimated.
  • Hands-on product testing and experience claims. FTC guidance bars claims about experience with a product that has not been tried S07 (PF-21). The marketing content therefore makes none.
  • Click and conversion tracking. Figures come from the owner’s Associates reports. No tag has been supplied (FLG-010), and no reports were available (FLG-011).
  • Automated optimisation. There is no campaign data to optimise yet. The generator rebuilds the site from the data files once verified data is added S20.

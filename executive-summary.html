As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. How we work

Research workspace: this page retains dated material from earlier passes. Current implementation status · Current measurement definition · Historical verification limits.

Executive summary

A plain-language summary of what can and cannot be verified today. Each finding links to its official source.

Bottom line

Headline: all five submitted short links each open a live Amazon product page, three of them air fryers and two of them meat thermometers. Their nine-character codes are not ASINs, and the destinations are not confirmed as the submitted products FLG-001 FLG-002 FLG-027 CBB-F-01. Product name, brand, price and rating for the submitted links are left blank. A tracking ID (shopnow047c-20) was supplied on 2026-11-17 but is unverified and appears in no link CBB-F-02.

Read this first (2026-11-17): two of the five — 5B-F-01 — are meat thermometers in a different Amazon browse node from the three air-fryer listings this site was built around, and they appear in no other data file in this repository 5B-F-02. Before redesigning anything, read the product-intent confirmation record, the open questions and the three paths. The same two links are recorded independently by session 82372fd1 on the intent-record page, where their observed fields are still empty.

Price display, API-derived reviews, link-model-specific earnings, traffic, clicks, conversions and niche profitability are not represented as live or established. Point-in-time observations are kept in the research log. Any value not confirmed through an eligible official route remains blank and is flagged.

Key findings

  • All five short links each open a live Amazon product page. The first three destinations were read on 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14 S11 · S12 · S13 · S14 · S15 · S16 and re-read on 2026-11-17 S76 · S77 · S78; the two links added on 2026-11-17 were read that day S70 · S71 · S72 · S73 and open meat thermometers rather than air fryers F-2F-01. The nine-character codes are not in the 10-character ASIN form S06, and the direct amazon.com/dp pages for them return Page Not Found S08 · S09 · S10. The destinations are not confirmed as the submitted products FLG-001 FLG-002 FLG-027.
  • A tracking ID was supplied but is not yet usable. The owner's 2026-11-17 brief names shopnow047c-20 as the Selected Tracking ID; it is recorded in config/platform.json and matches the repository's tag pattern, but assignment to an Associates account cannot be confirmed without a logged-in Associates console, and no link on the site carries it CBB-F-02 S02.
  • The short links resolve to other products. Each link ended on a different product page, with the parameters lv=shuf and plpRedirect=mhFallback. The mapping was the same on two fetches on 2026-11-13 and again on 2026-11-14. These are recorded as observed only FLG-002 S11 · S12 · S13.
  • Applying the shortener rules to link.amazon needs a judgement call. The policies say a shortener must not make the Amazon destination unclear S02 (PF-06), a link must not be cloaked or obscured (6(v)), and purchases through a link that sends users on indirectly, without an affirmative click, are disqualified as a Redirecting Link S30 (CIS 2(e)). No document names link.amazon; what its redirects actually do is a behaviour observation, and it has to be tested in a logged-in Associates account FLG-003.
  • Category rates are public; per-product rates are not assignable. Amazon’s Standard Commission Income Statement, Table 1, is readable without a sign-in (Kitchen 4.50 %, Home 3.00 %, All Other Categories 4.00 %) S25 · S26, and was re-read on 2026-11-14. The per-product rate cannot be stated, because the listing’s category is unverified and the account-specific rate card route redirects to sign-in S03 FLG-005.
  • A category page now exists. Air Fryers shows the verified category path and a six-position bestseller benchmark, each row linked to its official page S43. Prices and ratings are withheld under the display rules FLG-022 FLG-025.
  • Automated product data needs Creators API access. The official prerequisite is “at least 10 qualifying sales within the past 30 days” S04. Prices and reviews may be shown only through a Special Link or the API S02 (PF-04, PF-05, PF-44, PF-45) FLG-006 FLG-022 FLG-023.
  • The AI layer needs written approval from Amazon. The Creators API terms bar analysing, aggregating or repurposing its data, and bar using Program Content to develop or train machine learning or language models, without express prior written approval S02 (PF-23, PF-24, PF-25) FLG-016.
  • Eligibility for a Norway-based owner is not stated. Schedule 1 of the Operating Agreement lists only the United States site, amazon.com S01 (PF-35) FLG-015.
  • PA-API 5 is deprecated. The official notice says it is being replaced by the Creators API and that calls now return HTTP 403 AccessDeniedException S05 (PF-17), re-read on 2026-11-14. Another session branch cites PA-API 5 as the sanctioned source FLG-007.
  • Disclosure is on every page. The required wording is quoted from the Operating Agreement, Section 5 S01 (PF-01). Placement for FTC purposes still needs owner review FLG-013.

What was built

  • A GitHub Pages site (this site) with a landing dashboard and category pages: Products, Pricing & commission, Reviews & competition, Content & compliance, Campaigns & tracking.
  • A Flagged for review page with 187 items, each with its evidence sources and the action needed.
  • The earlier session’s site from PR #1 is merged in. Its scripts, data and HTML are archived under archive/26634cd5/, and its old analysis.html and category URLs redirect to the pages that replace them.
  • A Sources page that lists each official page used, its retrieval status, and what it supports.
  • A data layer (data/*.json) with a generator (scripts/build_site.py) and a checker (scripts/check_site.py). The checker fails if a table row has no official source link, or if a value appears without a source.
  • An independent re-verification pass on 2026-11-14 (this session): 90 official sources, every load-bearing claim re-fetched and compared. Two earlier statements were found overstated and corrected rather than quietly dropped — see V-F1 and V-F2 on the Flagged for review page.

What is not done, and why

  • Product research, niche identification, competition and commission-potential analysis. These need product data, which needs verified identifiers (FLG-001) and Creators API access (FLG-006). Nothing was estimated.
  • SEO articles, comparisons and buying guides. Product claims must be accurate (PF-10), and the first-hand experience the FTC describes (PF-21) has not happened. These are blocked (FLG-012).
  • Click and conversion tracking. Figures come from the owner’s Associates reports. A tag value was supplied on 2026-11-17 but is unverified and appears in no link (CBB-F-02, FLG-010), and no reports were available (FLG-011).
  • Automated optimisation. There is no data to optimise. The generator can rebuild the site once verified data is added.

Owner items (flagged; they do not block the merge)

  • Give the intended product URL or 10-character ASIN for each of the five products (FLG-001, CBB-F-01, 0C4-F-01); the product-intent record on Products is where the confirmation lands, and the register to fill in is on the five-link intake page.
  • Test each submitted link in a logged-in Associates account and report whether clicks are tracked (FLG-002, FLG-003, 0C4-F-02).
  • Confirm shopnow047c-20 is the tag assigned to this Associates account, then run one Special Link per product before scaling (CBB-F-02, decision-queue CBB-Q-01, 0C4-F-04); the tag value is now on file but unverified, not a password or token (FLG-010).
  • Confirm which Amazon product category each of the five listings is in, so the public Table 1 rate can be applied to it (FLG-005, F-2F-04; breadcrumbs observed on S14–S16, S60–S62, S72, S73 but the commission-category assignment stays open). The category rates themselves are already published and verified; what is missing is the mapping from a listing's browse node to a Table 1 category.
  • Decide the site scope: five products across two categories, or air fryers only (CBB-Q-03, 0C4-F-03 / 0C4-Q-16).

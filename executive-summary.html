Key findings

  • 3 of 3 links resolve to real listings, stably. Four observations over two days (two sessions) map each code to the same listing every time. Control tests show the shortener rejects unregistered codes FLG-022 FLG-024 S11 · S12 · S13 · S30 · S31 · S32.
  • 0 of 3 submitted codes are ASINs. ASINs are 10 characters; the codes are 9, and amazon.com/dp/<code> returns “Page Not Found” FLG-001 S06 · S08 · S09 · S10.
  • Prices and ratings are dated observed snapshots. Captured from Amazon's own result pages on 2026-11-14. They are volatile deal prices, and Participation Requirements 2(b) and 6(t) restrict how a live Associates site may display prices and star ratings FLG-023 S33 · S34 · S35 · S02 (PF-04, PF-05).
  • Commission rates cannot be verified. The rate schedule redirected to a sign-in page on 2026-11-13 S03 FLG-005. Category reference rates exist (Kitchen 4.50%) but the listings' commission category is not documented FLG-021 S25.
  • Associates compliance of link.amazon is unconfirmed. The policies say a shortener must not make the Amazon destination unclear S02 (PF-06), and purchases from a Redirecting Link are disqualified (PF-07). Whether these links carry an Associates tag is unverified FLG-003 FLG-010.
  • Automated product data needs Creators API access. The official prerequisite is “at least 10 qualifying sales within the past 30 days” S04. PA-API 5 is deprecated and being replaced by the Creators API S05 (PF-17) FLG-006 FLG-007.
  • The AI layer needs written approval from Amazon. The Program Policies bar analysing, aggregating or repurposing API data, and bar using Program Content to develop or train machine learning or language models, without express prior written approval S02 (PF-23, PF-24, PF-25) FLG-016.
  • Enrolment and eligibility are gated. Schedule 1 lists only the United States site S01 (PF-35); the application needs about 3 qualifying sales in 180 days S29 (PF-40), and 180 days of no referred sale risks dormancy S28 (PF-41) FLG-015.
  • Disclosure is on every page. The required wording is quoted verbatim from Operating Agreement Section 5 and was re-read on 2026-11-14 S01 · S36 (PF-01). FTC guidance (Disclosures 101, re-read 2026-11-14) requires the disclosure to be hard to miss S07 · S38 (PF-20) FLG-013.
  • Marketing content is published, facts-only. Product briefs, a comparison and a buying guide use only verified listing attributes with source links, state no prices or ratings, and claim no first-hand experience S02 · S07 (PF-10, PF-21) FLG-012.

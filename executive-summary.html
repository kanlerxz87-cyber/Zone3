Executive summary

A plain-language summary of what can and cannot be verified today. Each finding links to its official source.

Bottom line

The three Amazon links in the brief cannot be verified as product listings. Their identifiers are nine characters long. Amazon’s official Creators API documentation shows ASIN values of ten characters S06. The amazon.com product page for each identifier returns “Page Not Found” S08 · S09 · S10. All of this was re-checked on 2026-11-14 and reproduced exactly; see the re-verification table on the dashboard. The short links open Amazon product pages, but for three different products (two CHEFMAN air fryers and one Emeril Lagasse air fryer toaster oven), which are not confirmed as the submitted products S11 · S12 · S13 · S14 · S15 · S16.

As the brief requires, every product name, price, rating, review count, commission rate, niche score and click or conversion figure is left blank and marked Unverified. No value was inferred.

Key findings

  • All three supplied short links resolve to live product listings. Their nine-character tokens are short-link identifiers, not ASINs; a direct /dp/<token> 404 does not mean the short link fails. S30 · S31 · S32 Dated verification log.
  • The short links resolve to other products. Each link ended on a different product page, with the parameters lv=shuf and plpRedirect=mhFallback. The mapping was the same on two fetches on 2026-11-13 and again on 2026-11-14. These are recorded as observed only FLG-002 S11 · S12 · S13.
  • Applying the shortener rules to link.amazon needs a judgement call. The policies say a shortener must not make the Amazon destination unclear S02 (PF-06), a link must not be cloaked or obscured (6(v)), and purchases through a link that sends users on indirectly, without an affirmative click, are disqualified as a Redirecting Link S30 (CIS 2(e)). No document names link.amazon; what its redirects actually do is a behaviour observation, and it has to be tested in a logged-in Associates account FLG-003.
  • Category rates are public; per-product rates are not assignable. Amazon’s Standard Commission Income Statement, Table 1, is readable without a sign-in (Kitchen 4.50 %, Home 3.00 %, All Other Categories 4.00 %) S25 · S26, and was re-read on 2026-11-14. The per-product rate cannot be stated, because the listing’s category is unverified and the account-specific rate card route redirects to sign-in S03 FLG-005.
  • Automated product data needs Creators API access. The official prerequisite is “at least 10 qualifying sales within the past 30 days” S04. Prices and reviews may be shown only through a Special Link or the API S02 (PF-04, PF-05) FLG-006.
  • The AI layer needs written approval from Amazon. The Creators API terms bar analysing, aggregating or repurposing its data, and bar using Program Content to develop or train machine learning or language models, without express prior written approval S02 (PF-23, PF-24, PF-25) FLG-016.
  • Eligibility for a Norway-based owner is not stated. Schedule 1 of the Operating Agreement lists only the United States site, amazon.com S01 (PF-35) FLG-015.
  • PA-API 5 is deprecated. The official notice says it is being replaced by the Creators API and that calls now return HTTP 403 AccessDeniedException S05 (PF-17), re-read on 2026-11-14. Another session branch cites PA-API 5 as the sanctioned source FLG-007.
  • Disclosure is on every page. The required wording is quoted from the Operating Agreement, Section 5 S01 (PF-01). Placement for FTC purposes still needs owner review FLG-013.

What was built

  • A GitHub Pages site (this site) with a landing dashboard and category pages: Products, Pricing & commission, Reviews & competition, Content & compliance, Campaigns & tracking.
  • A Flagged for review page with 26 items, each with its evidence sources and the action needed.
  • The earlier session’s site from PR #1 is merged in. Its scripts, data and HTML are archived under archive/26634cd5/, and its old analysis.html and category URLs redirect to the pages that replace them.
  • A Sources page that lists each official page used, its retrieval status, and what it supports.
  • A data layer (data/*.json) with a generator (scripts/build_site.py) and a checker (scripts/check_site.py). The checker fails if a table row has no official source link, or if a value appears without a source.
  • An independent re-verification pass on 2026-11-14 (this session): 32 official sources, every load-bearing claim re-fetched and compared. Two earlier statements were found overstated and corrected rather than quietly dropped — see V-F1 and V-F2 on the Flagged for review page.

What is not done, and why

  • Product research, niche identification, competition and commission-potential analysis. These need product data, which needs verified identifiers (FLG-001) and Creators API access (FLG-006). Nothing was estimated.
  • SEO articles, comparisons and buying guides. Product claims must be accurate (PF-10), and the first-hand experience the FTC describes (PF-21) has not happened. These are blocked (FLG-012).
  • Click and conversion tracking. Figures come from the owner’s Associates reports. No tag has been supplied (FLG-010), and no reports were available (FLG-011).
  • Automated optimisation. There is no data to optimise. The generator can rebuild the site once verified data is added.

Owner items (flagged; they do not block the merge)

  • Give the intended product URL or 10-character ASIN for each of the three products (FLG-001).
  • Test each submitted link in a logged-in Associates account and report whether clicks are tracked (FLG-002, FLG-003).
  • Confirm which Amazon product category each of the three listings is in, so the public Table 1 rate can be applied to it (FLG-005, FLG-021). The category rates themselves are already published and verified.
  • Confirm Associates enrolment and supply the Associates tag, not a password or token (FLG-010).

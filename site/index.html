What this comparison deliberately leaves out

Price, star rating, review count, per-product commission, trend rank and conversion rate. Prices and ratings need an Amazon-served link or API data (Program Policies 2(b) and 6(t)). Per-product commission is not published by category-to-ASIN anywhere Amazon makes public. Trend and conversion figures have no official Amazon endpoint at all — the Creators API exposes four operations, none of them a trend or conversion report. Filling those cells would mean inventing them. See the sources.

How this page is made

  1. Read the listing, not a summary of it. Every specification on this page comes from the listing title and breadcrumb on the page itself, fetched 2026-11-16.
  2. Check it against the rules before publishing. Prices, ratings, claims and disclosures are governed by Amazon's Operating Agreement and Program Policies and by the FTC's disclosure guidance. Each rule that shaped this page is quoted on the evidence page with a link.
  3. Leave the unknown empty, and say why. A blank cell here carries a sentence explaining what would fill it. A dash would look tidier and tell you less.
  4. Publish the audit trail. Every row on the evidence page names the page it was read from and the date it was read.

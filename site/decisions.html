Fix the front door: one clear home page with a single job

D-01 implemented

Implemented on /site/ in this session as a proposal preview so it can be judged by looking at it. Reversible: the page is new and nothing else was changed.

The problem
The repository root opens on a 19-item navigation bar whose first screen is an audit dashboard: six KPI tiles, then 17 navigation tiles, then a site map and a 12-row re-verification table. A reader who arrived to decide which air fryer to buy cannot find that decision anywhere on the first screen. That is the main reason the site reads as internal tooling rather than a marketing site.
What is proposed
Give the root a reader-first home page: a short promise, the three products as cards with what the listing actually states, one primary action, and a visible provenance line. Move the audit dashboard to its own page (it already exists at /dashboard/) and link it once as 'How this was verified'.
Evidence
  • ObservedThe current root page is an audit dashboard with 17 navigation tiles and a re-verification table. (Read of index.html in this repository, 2026-11-16.)
  • OfficialI2-S01 A disclosure must be clearly and prominently stated on the site.
  • OfficialI2-S06 A disclosure is likely to be missed if it appears only in a footer or on an about page.
Effort
Low — one page template, no new data.
Expected effect
Editorial: converts first-time readers instead of losing them to an audit table. No traffic or conversion figure is claimed.
Risks
If the root is replaced, the audit pages must still be reachable in one click, or the verification record becomes hidden.
What it will not do
It does not add a price, a rating, a trend claim or a working buy link. It does not change any figure.

What is proposed

Measure before growing: a first-party ledger of five defined events

D-16 implemented

Implemented 2026-11-17 (session ded63349): assets/site.js records five events into one localStorage key; site/measure.html defines them, shows the device-side counts and exports CSV. No third-party script was added.

The problem
The brief asks for click and conversion measurement before anything else is written. The site had no counting at all, so the next guide topic could only be chosen by instinct, and no one could tell whether any guide was read.
What is proposed
Record five events — page_view, outbound_product_click, comparison_open, guide_read_through, decision_open — in one localStorage key on the visitor's own device, capped at 500 rows, with no cookies, no identifiers, no referrer storage and no network call. The measurement page shows the device-side counts and exports CSV.
Evidence
  • OfficialI2-S02 Participation Requirements 6(s) bans artificially generated clicks or sessions; the ledger counts real clicks by a real visitor on this site's own pages and never creates an Amazon session.
  • OfficialI2-S02 2(b) bans associating a sub-tag or ID with a specific end user; the ledger stores no per-person identifier and never assigns per-user tags.
  • OfficialI2-S02 6(y) bars price tracking and alerting on an Associate's Site; the ledger records click events only.
  • EditorialThe five event names and thresholds are this site's own definitions, chosen to answer one question per event; they are not a claim about traffic, demand or any official standard. (Definition adopted on site/measure.html, 2026-11-17.)
Effort
Small — one JS block, one page, no infrastructure.
Expected effect
The next guide topic can be chosen from measured opens and read-throughs instead of instinct, and every later claim about the site's traffic has a defined, checkable meaning.
Risks
Device-side counts are per-browser and lost when a visitor clears storage; they are a floor, not a census. Nothing on the site presents them as site-wide numbers.
What it will not do
It does not measure conversions (Amazon-side reports only, gated behind the account), does not identify anyone, and does not send data anywhere.
Depends on

Air fryer oven vs basket fryer: the 26QT is not a bigger basket

Two of the three machines here are baskets you pull out. The third is a countertop oven with two side-hinged doors, a convection cavity and accessories that come in the box. If you read it as "the big one" you will buy the wrong shape of machine.

The category filing is the interesting part

Amazon runs a separate browse node for Air Fryer Ovens and still lists this 26QT machine under Air Fryers. Two practical consequences for anyone shopping this category:

  • Browsing a category is not comparing like with like. The Air Fryers node mixes basket machines and oven machines, so a list of "top air fryers" can be a list of two different products in disguise. Sort by what opens how, not by rank.
  • Category membership is not a spec. Where a machine sits in Amazon's browse tree says nothing about temperature, power or cavity size. It is a merchandising decision, and it moves.

One dated observation, offered as an observation: on 2026-11-16 this 26QT listing appeared at position #6 on Amazon's own "Best Sellers in Air Fryers" page for node 17659096011. That page says "Updated frequently", so the number is a snapshot of a page on a date — not a quality claim, and not a reason to buy. CBA-S06

What an oven buys you that a basket does not

  • Height. A cavity takes a tall dish; a basket's usable depth ends at its wall.
  • Racks and trays instead of one basket floor. "Six cooking accessories included" is the listing's own way of saying the cooking surface is configurable.
  • Preset breadth. "24 One-Touch Preset Options" is a different proposition from a dial with time and temperature — whether you will ever use 24 of them is a question about you, not about the machine.

What a basket buys you that an oven does not

  • Shake-and-go. A basket you can pull, shake and slide back is a one-handed action mid-cook; doors and racks are not.
  • Two things, two settings. Only P1 has that: two independent 3QT baskets and a Sync Finish control that exists to bring both to the table together.
  • Smaller storage footprint. We cannot quantify this: exterior dimensions for the oven are not in the text we read, so this page does not guess them.

What this page will not claim

That rotisserie or dual-speed fan features are on the machine today. The listing's own URL slug still reads "Emeril-Multi-Function-functions-dual-speed-rotisserie" — the words dual-speed and rotisserie — while the title served today does not name them. We are not going to pick a winner between the two. Check the current listing before you rely on either feature. CBA-S02

That it is cheaper to run, faster, or crispier. All three would need either a test we have not run (and the FTC's rule is that you cannot describe an experience with a product you have not tried) or figures these listing lines do not state.

Any price, star rating or review count. Not publishable here under Amazon's Participation Requirements 2(b) and 6(t) unless Amazon serves the data in the link or it comes from the Creators API. CBA-S09

Decide in three questions

  1. Do you want one appliance to replace a toaster oven as well? If yes, the oven-style machine is the only one of the three that claims that job in its own title.
  2. Do you mostly reheat and crisp single foods? Basket. Faster to load, less to store.
  3. Do you cook for more than two people most nights? Then read the capacity page before this one, and treat the 24 presets as a nice-to-have rather than the reason.

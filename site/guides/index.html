Why 5 guides are live and 3 do not

A guide goes live once every sentence in it traces to a listing or an official page, the house lint passes, and each source link has been re-read the same day. The Held rows are held because the question needs either a number the listings do not state or a test nobody has run; a row marked “will not publish” is a topic this site is not permitted to write, and it stays listed here rather than being quietly dropped. Every page carries its reviewer note: an agent research desk, no human editor yet (question Q-5 on the open-questions register, decision D-10 on the board).

The rule every guide follows

Five rules, and the sixth one the build enforces mechanically (tools/cba81fa5_lint.py, documented in the house style):

  • One question. If a page answers three questions it answers none of them well.
  • Facts with sources. Every specification links the listing or official page it was read from, on the row it supports.
  • No invented experience. No “we tested”, no “in our kitchen”, no implied ownership.
  • No numbers we cannot publish. No price, star rating or review count until Amazon serves or supplies them.
  • Arithmetic labelled as arithmetic. A ratio we worked out is shown as ours, never as Amazon’s.
  • A named gap. Each guide ends by stating what it cannot tell you and where to check it yourself.

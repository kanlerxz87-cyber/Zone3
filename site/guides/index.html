Why only one guide is live

A guide is only published here once every sentence in it can be traced to a listing or an official page. The other three are drafted from the same verified facts and are waiting on the editorial pass described in D-10 — a named human editor — because a page with no editor is exactly the page that reads as generated.

The rule every guide follows

  • One question. If a page answers three questions it answers none of them well.
  • Facts with sources. Every specification links the listing it came from.
  • No invented experience. No “we tested”, no “in our kitchen”, no implied ownership.
  • No numbers we cannot publish. No price, star rating or review count until Amazon serves or supplies them.
  • A named gap. Each guide ends by stating what it cannot tell you and where to check it yourself.

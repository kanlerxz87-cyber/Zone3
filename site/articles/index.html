Field notes: the article library

Each article in this library answers one question, using only what the manufacturer's Amazon listing states — and it names what the listing leaves out. No price, no star rating, no “we tried it”: not because those details are boring, but because this site is not allowed to publish them until Amazon serves them through an approved path, and honest limits are more useful than invented confidence.

Listing facts on every page below were read from the official pages on 2026-11-16. Explanations of cooking terms are marked as general background, not listing facts.

The rules these pages follow

  1. Listing facts carry their source. Every product claim links to the exact listing page it was read from, with the date it was read. The full register lives on the evidence page.
  2. Blanks say why they are blank. Prices and star ratings are governed by Amazon's Participation Requirements 2(b) and 6(t) and stay unpublished until Amazon serves them through an approved path; anything merely missing from a listing is called out as missing. See the rule, quoted.
  3. No borrowed experience. This site has not cooked with these machines, and saying otherwise would breach the FTC's rule against describing experience you do not have.
  4. General explanations are labelled. Cooking-term background is marked as general knowledge, separate from what a listing states.

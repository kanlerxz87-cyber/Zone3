How we measure — five events, one ledger, no third parties

Before this site grows, it measures. This page defines the five events the site counts, states exactly what the counter stores and never stores, and sets the publishing cadence each new guide enters. The counting machinery is deliberately small and it lives on your device — this page can be checked line by line, like everything else here.

Adopted 2026-11-17 · policy facts the rules below quote were read on 2026-11-16 · implemented as proposals D-16, D-17 and D-22

The five defined events

The five events are this site's own definitions — an editorial choice, not a fact about any official standard, and not a claim about how much traffic the site receives. Amazon publishes no traffic, conversion or popularity endpoint an affiliate site could cite, so none is claimed here. What each event is for:

What the ledger stores, and what it never stores

The ledger is a few lines of JavaScript in assets/site.js, on this device only. Stored per event: the event name, a UTC timestamp, the page path, and — for product clicks — which product card (P1–P3). Kept to the most recent 500 events.

The publishing cadence

The cadence is an editorial policy adopted on 2026-11-17 (D-17) — a commitment this site makes to itself, not a fact quoted from any external page. Each new guide enters the sitemap and the RSS feed automatically at build time; nothing is listed by hand.

What measurement cannot do yet

  • Conversions cannot be measured from this site. Reported orders exist only in Amazon's Associates reports for the account holder, behind a sign-in no agent can reach. When a tracking ID exists, reported orders are transcribed into the campaign ledger from those first-party reports, dated and sourced — never forecast.
  • No server-side endpoint exists. GitHub Pages is static hosting; it cannot run an endpoint. Hosting one is the owner's infrastructure decision — recorded, with the options, as D-20. Until then the ledger is device-side by design, which is also why it is privacy-preserving by construction.
  • Search queries are not visible yet. Connecting the site to a Search Console property needs the owner's verification, recorded as D-21. Until then, no claim is made about which queries bring readers here.
  • No traffic, trend or popularity claim appears anywhere on this site. Amazon publishes no such data to affiliates, and every number this site does not have stays blank on purpose. I2-S02

Where this is implemented

The ledger lives in assets/site.js (build source: tools/build_i2_site.py, event names and limits visible in the source); this page is site/measure.html; the cadence is recorded in the decision console as D-17 and in the repository's coordination log. The product-intent record is the companion register for the links themselves.

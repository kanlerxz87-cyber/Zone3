What has to be true before a click can earn anything?Every link must carry the Associates tag (format XXXXX-##) as a URL parameter, and Special Links must use the Associates ID Amazon assigned. Is the site eligible to join at all?Enrollment requires a complete application, a site that is publicly available at the address given, and original content — content using third-party material "must contain significant commentary, analysis, or transformation". What are the payout mechanics if it works?Commission is paid in the site's default currency roughly 60 days after the end of the month it was earned. What must the disclosure say, and where?The exact sentence "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." is required by the Operating Agreement, and violating that section is treated as a material breach. The FTC adds placement: disclose the relationship, "place it so it's hard to miss", and put it with the endorsement itself — not only on an about page or below a fold.Every page here carries the sentence in a banner above the content and again in the footer near the links. Any redesign keeps that pattern or has to justify dropping it.CBA-S12 S01

3. Which path the answers choose

Path A — stay a research-grade referenceShip what is here: verified attributes, four guides, blanks for the unknown. Needs: nothing further. Buys: credibility and a defensible record. Costs: no revenue, and traffic that cannot be measured.
Path B — become earn-readyQ-1 (tag) then Q-4 (API) in that order. The tag turns links into Special Links; the API makes prices, availability, ratings and compliant comparison tables possible. Needs: two owner inputs and a secret store. Buys: measurable clicks, orders, revenue, and the automated research the brief actually asks for. Costs: nothing on the content side — the copy already avoids everything that would break.
Path C — grow traffic before monetisingMore guides per week under the same lint, more products in the same category, no tag. Needs: Q-5 (editor) and Q-9 (topic order). Buys: search surface area. Costs: publishing volume without a human reviewer is precisely how a site earns the reputation this brief is trying to lose.

Recommended path: B, with A's discipline kept intact. Nothing in the pipeline moves until Q-1 is answered, and Q-1 is one value. Until then the site should keep shipping exactly the way it shipped today: sourced claims, labelled arithmetic, blanks where a source is silent, and a disclosure a reader cannot miss. Path C before B means growing an audience for links that cannot earn or be measured.

The feature that looks free and is not

A price-drop alert is the most-requested feature in affiliate content and the most common cause of trouble, because it is explicitly named in the Participation Requirements. The workaround adopted here keeps the analytically useful half: prices are fetched, timestamped and used to rank and to decide whether a 'deal' claim may be made, and each price is re-pulled within the 24-hour cache limit — but no watchlist, no alert subscription and no price-history chart is exposed to readers.

The comparison constraint is the other easy one to get wrong. Any comparison layout must show the lowest new price and, where Amazon provides it, the lowest used price. That is why the price sheet above has separate lowest_new and lowest_used columns rather than a single price, and why the comparison template will refuse to render if either is missing.

Reading the constraints

These ten rows are the part of niche analysis that can be done from official documents alone, and they are the ones that most often invalidate an otherwise attractive niche. Two are underrated: pre-release / pre-order products are disqualified, which structurally kills launch-hype content, and subscriptions are disqualified, which kills recurring-product niches whose apparent LTV looks excellent.

The original-content bar is the other practical constraint: the Site must contain original content, and reuse of third-party material must add "significant commentary, analysis, or transformation". For an AI pipeline this sets the quality floor — bulk restating of Amazon listing copy is a rejection risk, not a shortcut. Product-list pages (search results, Prime Day events, department homepages) additionally require original content relevant to the link.

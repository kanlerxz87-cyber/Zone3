The prompt

Copy this prompt only after reviewing the questions and choosing the proposed page set. The five observed listing destinations are not confirmations of product intent. Event names, brand direction, cadence and architecture below are proposals; never turn them into facts.

ROLE AND GOAL

Design and build a polished, reader-first Amazon affiliate publication for people comparing kitchen appliances. The goal is a useful shopping decision, not a compliance dashboard. Preserve the existing evidence discipline, make missing facts visible without turning the homepage into an audit, and never promise search rank, sales or income.

AUDIENCE AND PRODUCT SCOPE

Use a provisional reader: someone comparing a countertop kitchen appliance and wanting a clear question answered before leaving for the listing. Do not infer the target country, audience demographics, currency or marketplace from the owner’s current location or the observed Amazon.com destinations. The site currently spans air fryers and meat thermometers; present them as separate categories until the owner confirms the broader niche.

----------------------------------------------------------------

BRAND AND VOICE

Use “Zone3” only as the current working label. Final publisher name, domain/handle clearance, palette and voice are not confirmed. Write like a careful human editor: direct, specific, calm, and willing to say what a listing does not tell you. Avoid generic AI phrases, stacked hedges, fake urgency, superlatives without evidence and fictional first-person experience. Use a human byline only when the named person has actually reviewed the page.

----------------------------------------------------------------

INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE — RECOMMENDED DEFAULT, NOT YET SELECTED

Recommended public structure: one root publication front page; /shop/ for distinct product/category destinations (Air fryers and Meat thermometers); /guides/ for question-led editorial; one clear “Research & governance” navigation group and a footer that keeps sources, decisions, flagged items and the campaign ledger reachable. Inventory unique content in /site/, /read/, /marketing/ and /articles/ before merging, archiving or redirecting anything. Do not create another parallel shop or blog.

----------------------------------------------------------------

VISUAL SYSTEM

Design direction: editorial kitchen journal, not admin dashboard. Warm paper/cream background, deep ink, restrained olive/sage and a single warm accent; editorial serif for display headings paired with a legible sans-serif for body copy; comfortable 17px body type, generous whitespace and a readable line length. Cards should feel like a magazine shelf, not KPI tiles. Use visible focus, semantic headings, adequate contrast, reduced-motion support and comfortable touch targets. The palette is a proposal until the publisher identity is chosen.

----------------------------------------------------------------

BUYER-FACING COMPONENTS

Build a compact two-category chooser; product cards that show the official listing title, observed/intended status and a useful question; a comparison that includes only attributes verified from official pages; and a short “what to check before you choose” list. Every product card must say what the listing states and what it does not establish. Do not issue an overall winner or “fits you if” claim without a human-reviewed basis.

----------------------------------------------------------------

AFFILIATE LINKS AND DISCLOSURES

On every page, put the exact disclosure “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” in a readable strip directly under the masthead and repeat it in the footer; keep disclosure near each recommendation. Mark every outbound product link rel="sponsored". State where the link goes. Do not silently replace the submitted short links or add a tracking redirect. The owner supplied shopnow047c-20, but do not embed it in rewritten links until account/marketplace and one Special Link destination/report test are verified. The supplied short links are unresolved, not declared invalid.

----------------------------------------------------------------

EVIDENCE RULES

Every product fact must link to the official page that supports it and retain a checked date. No official source means blank/“not verified”, with a visible flag. Never merge observed redirects with intended-product confirmation. Do not infer price, availability, rating, review count, popularity, trends, commission potential, conversion or revenue. Show price/rating/review content only through an approved Amazon-served link or permitted API data, after current policy and account access are verified. Do not send Amazon Program Content to AI/ML processing unless the current official policy and required written permission explicitly allow it.

----------------------------------------------------------------

EDITORIAL ENGINE AND TOPIC HYPOTHESES

Use one buyer question per page, answer directly, show the source, explain the decision trade-off and link to the relevant category/comparison. The four supplied working titles are: “Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?”, “A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity”, “How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs”, and “Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance.” Add separate thermometer questions only after confirming category scope and sources. These are editorial hypotheses, not evidence of search popularity.

----------------------------------------------------------------

SEASONS, BYLINES AND IMAGERY

Seasonal angles are hypotheses, never “trending” claims. Date each seasonal page, review it before the relevant season and maintain an expiry/recheck owner. Never invent an author, test, review or product experience. Do not use generated, scraped or copied product photography as genuine product photos. Use original owner-created photography with documented rights or a permitted Amazon image source; until rights/provenance are recorded, use typography and original diagrams instead.

----------------------------------------------------------------

ORGANIC DISCOVERY AND TOPIC MEASUREMENT

Build one distinct, useful guide per real buyer question. Give each page a unique title/description, canonical URL and purposeful internal links within its category. Keep the existing register-driven RSS feed and sitemap current on each publication. Verify the chosen site in Search Console and use dated query reports to choose later topics; no Search Console data is present yet. Do not guarantee rankings or claim a topic is popular until measured.

----------------------------------------------------------------

ANALYTICS EVENT PROPOSAL — NOT ACTIVE

The repository contains five event definitions in data/shop/measurement.json, explicitly marked PROPOSED because the brief asks for five events but does not name or approve them. Preserve them as a review draft, not live tracking: page_view (page path without query/hash, UTC day, protocol version); outbound_click (path, link host, and only an eligible last path segment; no query string, tag value, full URL, identity or Amazon conversion); guide_view (guide path/day); intent_view (intent-record path/day); flag_view (flagged/publication-pass path/day). The generated browser config currently has enabled=false and endpoint_url empty. The collector is a local script, not a hosted service. No event collection is evidenced.

----------------------------------------------------------------

PRIVACY AND ENDPOINT BOUNDARY

GitHub Pages serves static files and its official documentation says visitor IP addresses are logged. The repository includes a local collector prototype, but it is not hosted; counting remains OFF with an empty endpoint URL. A real request-handling runtime is separate. Do not claim privacy-preserving tracking or activate collection until the selected host's actual access/network logs, IP handling, retention, access controls and applicable notice/consent requirements are checked. Do not use cookies, persistent user IDs, fingerprinting, full referrers, query strings or full affiliate URLs. Use authorized Associates reports for order/conversion figures; no user-level Amazon conversion event is available to this page.

----------------------------------------------------------------

RELEASE AND COMPLIANCE GATE

Every page must retain the disclosure strip and footer copy; every outbound product link must be sponsored; every table row containing a fact must have an official source link or be explicitly blank/flagged. After any generator or route change, rebuild the feed and sitemap, crawl local links, run all repository compliance checks, and inspect the rendered pages on mobile. No deployment is complete if a gate fails. Keep the dashboard reachable through the one grouped navigation item and footer, not the buyer hero.

How to use this brief

  1. Review the current Questions and Suggestions pages first. They distinguish official facts, repository facts, owner inputs and proposed decisions.
  2. Confirm the five intended products and target marketplace in the enrolled Associates workflow; do not treat the five observed destinations as intent.
  3. After the owner confirms intended ASINs, generate and test one Associates Special Link per product in the enrolled workflow; check each destination and report attribution before changing product CTAs or scaling. Keep submitted short links unchanged and unresolved.
  4. Choose the canonical brand, category scope and route set. Recommended default is root + /shop/ + /guides/; map unique content and inbound links before archiving other layers.
  5. Name a human editor and document the testing and image-rights policy. Do not invent a byline, product test or product photography.
  6. Review the five-event proposal, select an endpoint host and inspect its official logging/retention documentation before any live collection. Keep Amazon orders report-sourced.
  7. Verify the canonical property and collect dated query data before describing a topic as popular or using it as the next editorial signal.
  8. Publish one approved question-led guide at the adopted cadence, with source links, clear disclosure, direct sponsored links, and feed/sitemap updates.
  9. Run the full release gates after each build; leave unavailable or unconfirmed values blank and publish a flagged item rather than guessing.

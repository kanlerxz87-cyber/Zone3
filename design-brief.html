The prompt

Copy everything in the box below and paste it into your design tool or coding agent. It is written to produce a reader-facing affiliate site, not a dashboard. The constraints block is not optional: each line cites the Amazon rule it comes from, and breaking one can disqualify commission or breach the Operating Agreement.

ROLE AND GOAL

You are designing and building a reader-facing Amazon affiliate website for air fryers. Its job is to help a real person choose between three specific air fryers, and to earn commission honestly when they click through to Amazon. It is NOT a data dashboard, an audit report, or an internal research tool. If a page would only make sense to the person who built it, cut it.

----------------------------------------------------------------

THE THREE PRODUCTS

Only three products exist. Do not invent others.
1. CHEFMAN 6 Quart Dual Basket Air Fryer Oven - two independent 3Qt nonstick dishwasher-safe baskets, Easy View windows, Sync Finish, Hi-Fry, auto shutoff, black. amazon.com/dp/B0CDY277D3
2. Emeril Lagasse 26QT French Door Multi-Function Air Fryer Toaster Oven - countertop convection cooker, 24 one-touch presets, extra-large interior, six cooking accessories included. amazon.com/dp/B09B7SB46R
3. Chefman Air Fryer 8 Qt TurboFry 4-in-1 - 450F Hi-Fry option, bake, dehydrate, frozen, XL nonstick dishwasher-safe basket, stainless steel. amazon.com/dp/B08DKYBTPH
These descriptions come from the listing titles only. Do not add specifications, prices, ratings or review counts.

----------------------------------------------------------------

AUDIENCE AND JOBS

Primary reader: a US home cook with limited counter space deciding whether to buy an air fryer this week, and if so which size. They arrive from Google with a question, want an answer in the first screen, and leave to check availability on Amazon. They are not interested in our verification process, our flag counts, or our data pipeline. Write for that person.

----------------------------------------------------------------

BRAND AND VOICE

Voice: a knowledgeable friend who owns too many kitchen gadgets. Direct, specific, willing to say which one to buy and which one not to. Short sentences. Concrete nouns and numbers instead of adjectives. No hedging stack, no 'in today's fast-paced world', no 'it's important to note', no 'unlock the power of', no em-dash pile-ups, no tricolon of adjectives, no concluding summary paragraph that restates the introduction. Never use the phrase 'AI-powered' about the site itself.

----------------------------------------------------------------

INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

Pages: Home (the decision, in one screen) - one Review page per product - one Comparison page (all three, side by side) - one Buying guide (evergreen, how to choose a size) - How we verify (the evidence layer: sources, blanks, flags) - About and contact. That is the whole site. Every nav label is a reader destination. No page is named after a process, a file, or a build.

----------------------------------------------------------------

DESIGN SYSTEM

Palette: warm off-white background #FAF7F2, near-black ink #16130F, ember accent #E4572E used only for actions and the recommended-product badge, sage #6B7A5A for secondary surfaces, hairline borders #E5DED3. No default blue. Type: a characterful display face for headlines (Fraunces or Instrument Serif) paired with Inter for body; body 17px/1.65, measure capped at 68ch; headlines tight at -0.02em. Layout: 1180px container, 24px gutters, generous vertical rhythm, asymmetric hero with text left and product visual right. Cards: 14px radius, 1px hairline border, no drop shadows except one soft lift on the primary CTA. Motion: none by default, 120ms ease on hover only.

----------------------------------------------------------------

COMPONENTS TO BUILD

Verdict card (one sentence, who it is for, who it is not for). Spec strip (capacity, configuration, presets, finish - listing-derived only). Sticky comparison table with the CTA column pinned on scroll. Who-should-buy-which decision list. Sourced-claim block, where each factual claim carries a small link to the Amazon page it came from. Disclosure block adjacent to every affiliate link. How-we-verify teaser linking to the evidence layer.

----------------------------------------------------------------

HARD CONSTRAINTS - AMAZON

- Every link to Amazon must carry the Associates tag as a URL parameter. Special Links must use the assigned Associates ID; a purchase is disqualified if it is not tracked because links were not properly formatted.
- The disclosure 'As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.' must appear clearly and prominently, adjacent to affiliate links, not only in the footer.
- Do not imply that Amazon sponsors, supports or endorses the site.
- Do not publish prices or availability unless Amazon serves the link or the data comes from Creators API or PA API. Do not scrape or hard-code a price.
- Do not publish star ratings or customer reviews unless obtained through Creators API or PA API.
- No price tracking or price-alerting feature.
- No link shortener that makes it unclear the link goes to Amazon. Do not cloak the destination.
- Any page linking to a product list, search results or department page must carry additional original content relevant to the link.
- No inaccurate, overbroad or misleading claim about any product or Amazon's prices.
- Do not promote non-Amazon products using Amazon Program Content.
- Amazon trademarks may not appear in the domain, subdomain, Associates tag or social handles.

----------------------------------------------------------------

HARD CONSTRAINTS - EVIDENCE

Every factual claim on the site links to the official page it was read from. Anything not confirmed from an official source is left blank and marked unverified; never fill it in, estimate it, or infer it. No trend rank, price, rating, review count or earnings estimate may appear without a source read on the day of publication.

----------------------------------------------------------------

HARD CONSTRAINTS - FTC

Disclose the affiliate relationship clearly and conspicuously, in the same place as the recommendation it applies to, in language a reader will actually see. Disclosure must not be buried below the fold or behind a link.

----------------------------------------------------------------

ANTI-SLOP RULES

No page named after a build artefact or an internal process. No headline statistic about our own completeness. No comparison table whose cells are mostly empty. No generic hero buttons. No three-column feature grid of vague benefits. No stock illustration of a kitchen. No fake urgency, no countdown, no 'as of today' price claims. Every article takes a position.

----------------------------------------------------------------

DELIVERABLE

A complete static site, responsive, accessible (WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, semantic headings, alt text), fast (no web fonts above 2 files, no third-party script), with a working sitemap and one clean page per item in the architecture above. Ship HTML and CSS that a human designer would not be embarrassed to have made.

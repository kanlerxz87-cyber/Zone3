Choosing an air fryer: the five decisions that actually differ

These are general buying considerations, not claims about any specific product on Amazon. They are listed so the reader knows what to check on the listing page themselves, where the current specification is authoritative.

  • Basket count. One basket means everything cooks at the same temperature. Two independently controlled baskets let a reader cook a main and a side without sequencing them.
  • Capacity against counter space. Capacity climbs from 6 QT to 26 QT across these three listings. Larger chambers generally need more counter depth; measure before ordering.
  • Form factor. A drawer-with-basket unit and a French-door oven unit cook differently. French doors plus a rotisserie suit larger items; a basket suits tossing and shaking.
  • Modes that matter to you. Dehydrate and bake appear in some listing titles and not others. If a reader will not use a mode, its presence should not drive the decision.
  • Cleaning. The listings that mention dishwasher-safe nonstick baskets are easier to live with day to day.

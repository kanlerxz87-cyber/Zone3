Why the product identity is still marked unconfirmed

The three supplied links are 9 characters long. Amazon defines an ASIN as a 10-character identifier S31, and a request for amazon.com/dp/<code> with each of the three returns Amazon's Page Not Found response S09. Each short link does open a live listing S11 · S12 · S13 · S14 · S15 · S16 — but three codes opening three different air fryers is exactly what a generic redirect looks like, so this site treats the mapping as observed, not as proof of what you meant to submit. Confirming this takes one line from you: the product names or the correct ASINs.

What this session did

  1. Intake of two new product links (0C4-T2). link.amazon/B0c8moW59 and link.amazon/B0f3GKalj were fetched on 2026-11-17, each observed to open one destination listing, each raw token re-checked against amazon.com/dp/<token> (Amazon's Page Not Found page in both cases), and each destination independently fetched to confirm title and category breadcrumb. Prices and star ratings visible on those pages were deliberately not recorded (Program Policies 2(b)/6(t)). Six sources (S70–S75) carry the observations.
  2. Product-intent record. Every one of the five submitted links now carries product_intent (intended ASIN, intended listing URL, confirmation date, source) — all blank until the owner confirms — strictly separated from the observed redirect target, published on Products.
  3. Special Links from the owner's tag (0C4-T3). data/special-links.json records shopnow047c-20 as owner-supplied and unvalidated; SL1–SL5 were built from the observed destinations and published. The tag is a public identifier by design (it must appear in every published link); no credentials are stored anywhere in this repository.
  4. Sponsored-link enforcement (0C4-T3). scripts/mark_sponsored.py adds rel="sponsored" to every outbound product anchor, runs as the last build step before the sitemap, and --check fails when a rebuild has dropped the marks; scripts/check_site.py check #10 fails when any /dp/ anchor lacks the attribute. The reader site's buy buttons switched from inactive to active tagged links with plain-language disclosure; I2-F-03 and Q1 were updated to match.
  5. Measurement layer (0C4-T4). Five defined events, a privacy contract (data/analytics.json), the browser client assets/track.js (DNT/GPC/Save-Data off-switch, payload {e,p,r,t}, no cookies, no identifiers) wired into all three page layers, a self-hosted reference endpoint tools/analytics_collector.py, and the Measurement page. The endpoint is not deployed (0C4-F-05) and the tracker sends nothing until an owner-controlled host exists.
  6. RSS feed (0C4-T5). scripts/build_feed.py builds feed.xml — 18 items across Research Desk (4), buying guides (5), field notes (3) and The Crisp Report (6) — with a --check gate; <link rel="alternate"> discovery is in every page head, the footer links the feed, robots.txt carries the Feed: hint.
  7. Register updates (0C4-T6). The executive summary carries a dated update for this session and recomputed counts (68 sources · 55 policy facts · 115 flags · 31 gate pages · 79 sitemap pages at that pass); Q1/757-M-01 are answered with the tag; SUG-05 moved from blocked to shipped-pending-validation; SUG-11…15 were published as proposals; questions 0C4-Q-16…18 and proposals 0C4-S-01…03 were added to the Review console.

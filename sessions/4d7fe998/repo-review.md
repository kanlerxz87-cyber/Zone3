## GitHub Pages applicability review

The current host is GitHub Pages and the publication contains affiliate product links and a buyer-facing product shortlist. GitHub’s official [Pages limits](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits), fetched 2026-11-19, say Pages is “not intended for or allowed to be used as a free web-hosting service” to run an online business, e-commerce site, or other site primarily directed at facilitating commercial transactions or providing commercial SaaS. The site is a reader/editorial publication that sends shoppers to Amazon rather than operating its own checkout; whether this particular affiliate-publication use meets that policy boundary is **not adjudicated by this audit**. A successful Pages build/deployment is not policy approval.

**Before scaling:** ask GitHub Support to assess the actual affiliate-publication use, or choose commercially suitable hosting and migrate before broadening commercial activity. Keep the static publication and the analytics collector as separate services. Do not claim either policy approval or a violation without an authoritative decision.

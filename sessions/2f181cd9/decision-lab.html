Decision Lab: review the suggestions, then choose

Thirteen proposals for the site, each with the paths available, the question that must be answered before a path can be chosen, and the source that makes the constraint real. Nothing on this page is implemented by publishing it.

How this console works

  1. Read the questions page first: it says what is known, what is not, and how to find out what is missing.
  2. Pick a path on each suggestion that offers more than one. A suggestion with an unanswered blocking question cannot be scheduled.
  3. Record the decision (suggestion id, path id, date, who decided) in the coordination log. The suggestion then moves to scheduled.
  4. Only then is it implemented, and the implementation row is filled in with the evidence pages that changed.
  5. Anything that cannot be verified from an official source stays blank and is published on the Flagged for review page instead of being filled with a guess.

Nothing here is implemented by publishing it. A suggestion moves to scheduled only when the owner records a decision, names the path chosen and dates it. The statuses are: proposed — Published for review. Not implemented. No work starts until the owner picks a path.; ready — No open blocker. Can be scheduled as soon as the owner says go; the path still needs choosing where more than one exists.; blocked — Cannot start. The blocking question is named in the row, and the row names how to unblock it.; rejected — Considered and not taken. The reason is recorded so it is not re-proposed next session..

SUG-2F-01 · Decide what this site is about now that five links span two categories

Scope blocked Needs the owner to confirm the intended product set (Q2F-01). Two of five links open meat thermometers, not air fryers.

What: Three submitted links open air-fryer listings (browse node 17659096011); the two new links open meat-thermometer listings (browse node 289810). The site's name, category pages, comparison tables and article plan are all written for air fryers only. Pick a scope before writing anything else, because every downstream page inherits it.

Why: A site whose navigation says 'Air Fryer Field Notes' and whose newest two products are thermometers reads as incoherent to a visitor and gives search engines no clear topic to rank.

Blocking question: Q2F-01

A · One publication, two category hubs

Keep one domain and one brand; add a second category hub (Kitchen Thermometers) beside the air-fryer hub, each with its own comparison and buying guide. Shared navigation: Shop, Guides, Compare, About.

Trade-off: Cheapest to run and keeps one domain's authority, but the brand name must stop naming a single appliance.

B · Two microsites

Split the thermometers onto their own domain with their own name and sitemap.

Trade-off: Cleanest topical signal for search, but doubles the publishing work and splits an already thin content base.

C · Air fryers only, for now

Keep the site as it is and hold the two thermometer links in the intake register, unpublished, until the owner confirms they were meant to be included.

Trade-off: Zero rework and no risk of promoting an unintended product, but leaves two submitted links unserved.

Effort: A: medium. B: large. C: none. · Risk: Publishing thermometers under an air-fryer brand without an owner decision would promote products that may not have been intended.

How we would know it worked: A dated owner decision recorded in COORDINATION.md, plus the scope statement appearing on the site's About page.

Constraint this must respect: S72 S73 S76 S14

## Defects found, in severity order

### 5D4-F01 — No outbound Amazon link carries the Associates tag (revenue-path defect)

- **Measured:** every outbound Amazon product anchor on the site is either the submitted
  `https://link.amazon/<9-char token>` or an untagged `https://www.amazon.com/dp/<ASIN>`.
  `grep -rho 'href="[^"]*tag=[^"]*"'` over all live pages returns nothing, and
  `tools/build_shop.py:780` *fails the build* if a `tag=` appears in a link.
- **Why that matters:** Program Policies, Participation Requirements 2(b): "you must include your
  Associates ID or 'tag' (appearing as XXXXX-## …) as a parameter in the URL of each link you place
  on your Site to an Amazon Site." Read 2026-11-19, source S02.
- **Consequence:** the site can send a reader to Amazon and earn nothing on the click.
- **Not a compliance conclusion:** the supplied `link.amazon` short links are **not** declared
  invalid here. Their status stays unresolved (FLG-003), as the brief instructs. What is proven is
  only that no link on this site carries the selected tracking ID.

### 5D4-F02 — Register-drift counts on the executive brief (false published numbers)

- **Measured:** `executive-brief.html` summary strip states **6** guides and **9** questions.
  The register (`data/publication.json`) holds **8** guides and **24** questions.
- **Also:** two paragraphs on `suggestions/index.html` say "the current six guides" (8 exist).
- **Cause:** literal numerals typed into `tools/build_publication.py` rather than derived, which is
  the exact failure mode an earlier session claimed to have fixed.
- **Fix direction:** every count derives from the register, and a gate fails if a typed count returns.

### 5D4-F03 — The reading room `six`/`seven` headings are typed even where the cards are not

- `review_round_html()` card counts *are* derived from their lists, but the headings above them
  ("Seven ways…", "The article slate: seven working titles.") are typed. Adding one row silently
  makes the heading false.

### 5D4-F04 — Front page and `/shop/` are competing duplicates

- **Measured:** with masthead/footer removed, `index.html` and `shop/index.html` are **65 %** the
  same words (1104 vs 1066). Both are `index,follow` and both are in `sitemap.xml`. They carry
  different `<title>`s over near-identical bodies — the classic pattern duplicate-content guidance
  warns about, on the two strongest URLs of the site.
- **Fix direction:** one canonical storefront. `/shop/` is the real catalogue; the root front page
  becomes a distinct front page, not a second copy of it.

### 5D4-F05 — Two indexable pages are orphans

- `shop/products/index.html` has **8** inbound internal links against 249 for
  `shop/air-fryers/`; the comparison table is the page a buyer needs to make a decision.
- `shop/meat-thermometers/index.html` is reachable, but the `shop/index.html` catalogue does not
  link the compare page in its own body copy (only in the masthead).

### 5D4-F06 — The product CTA and the "official listing" can point to different ASINs

- On each product card the button (`submitted_link`, i.e. `link.amazon/B04G1MEwY`) and the evidence
  link (`observed_listing_url`, i.e. `amazon.com/dp/B0CDY277D3`) are **separate destinations**. The
  page states they are the same product, but nothing in the build says so; intent is unconfirmed
  (FLG-002). A reader has two ways to leave the page for one product, and one of them is unverified
  as the intended product.

### 5D4-F07 — Supplier-listed performance wording is reproduced as if it were a fact

- `cleanup_title_wording` values such as "Nonstick dishwasher-safe baskets" appear in the
  comparison table under the header *Cleanup wording in title*. The column header is honest, but
  the visible values read as product capability. No test supports them, and Program Policies 2(b)
  requires content that is not "inaccurate, overbroad, deceptive or otherwise misleading" about a
  product.

### 5D4-F08 — Disclosure placement: present but not above the fold everywhere

- All 128 live pages contain the Associates sentence, but on the table-heavy pages the only
  in-header statement is inside the `details`/summaries; the strip is rendered after the masthead,
  which is compliant, yet `flagged.html` (281 KB) pushes it past 2,000 px of DOM.

### 5D4-F09 — `sitemap.xml` lists 16 pages while 4 indexable pages are missing `rel=alternate` feed link

- Minor: feed discovery works, but only some pages declare it.

### 5D4-F10 — The suggestions surface asks 24 questions but cannot record a decision

- `data/publication.json` carries 24 decision-readiness questions, 15 proposals, 5 paths — and
  the page offers a per-item 4-way `<select>` plus free-text answers. Nothing joins a question to
  the evidence that would close it, and nothing produces a dated decision record. The brief asks
  for "a section dedicated for suggestions so someone can review suggestions and choose the best
  path before implementing any changes"; the choosing half is a dead end.

## What this session will fix, and what it will not claim

Will fix: F01 (tag every outbound product link with the selected tracking ID, and keep the
submitted-link record intact and separate), F02/F03 (derive all counts), F04/F05 (one canonical
storefront, no orphan comparison page), F06 (one destination per product card, with the intent
record linked), F10 (a decision record surface where a choice produces a dated, exportable record).

Will **not** claim: that the tracking ID is enrolled on an account; that any link's attribution has
been validated in Associates Central; that any product fact beyond listing wording is true; that any
guide has traffic, demand or a ranking. Those stay flagged.

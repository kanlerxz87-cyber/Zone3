How a commission is actually earned

Read before modelling revenue: commission is a percentage of Qualifying Revenue, not of the sticker price.

Session window
Begins on Special Link click; ends at the earliest of (x) 24 hours elapse, (y) the customer places an order for a non-digital Product, or (z) the customer clicks through a Special Link that is not yours.
Add-to-cart window
Add-to-cart orders must be completed no later than 89 days after the initial click-through of the Special Link.
Fulfilment window
Within 180 days of purchase the Product must be shipped to, streamed or downloaded by, and paid for by the customer.
Qualifying Revenue
The amount Amazon actually receives from the Qualifying Purchase, less shipping charges, gift-wrapping fees, handling fees, taxes (e.g. sales tax and VAT), service charges, credits, rebates, credit card processing fees, and bad debt.

Three things make even the higher column an upper bound.

Commission is paid on Qualifying Revenue, not on the sticker price. Amazon defines Qualifying Revenue as the amount it actually receives, less shipping, gift wrapping, handling, taxes, service charges, credits, rebates, credit card processing fees and bad debt. For a discounted item that is below the price shown here, not above it.

The 24-hour session is short. A session begins on the link click and ends at the earliest of 24 hours elapsing, the customer placing an order for a physical product, or the customer clicking through someone else's tagged link. There is no long tail of attribution to rely on.

Add-to-cart only extends to 89 days. If the customer adds to cart but does not order, the order must be completed no later than 89 days after the initial click, and the item must then be shipped and paid for within 180 days of purchase.

Where the money actually is in this set

On these numbers the Emeril Lagasse unit is worth roughly 1.9 times a Chefman conversion at either rate, because the commission is a percentage of a price that is nearly double. That is why the demand data matters: if the higher-priced unit converts at even two-thirds the rate of the cheaper ones, it earns more per hundred clicks. Whether it does is unknowable without conversion data from a connected Associates account.

Bounty events are worth more than any of this

The rate table also lists fixed-dollar bounty events, and several dwarf the per-unit commission. A Prime free trial or paid membership pays $12.00. An Audible Premium Plus annual membership pays $25.00 and an Audible Standard monthly membership pays $20.00. A single Prime signup is worth roughly three to five air fryer conversions. Any content plan built only around the product commission is leaving the larger line item on the table.

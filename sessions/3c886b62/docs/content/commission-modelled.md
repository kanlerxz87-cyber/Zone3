
**These numbers are arithmetic, not facts.** They multiply a verified captured price by each of the two
candidate official commission rates. Amazon does not publish which rate applies to which ASIN, so neither
column is a prediction. They are shown together so the size of the unresolved question is visible.

| Product | Captured price | At 3.00% (Home row) | At 4.50% (Kitchen row) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CHEFMAN 6 qt dual basket | $79.93 | $2.40 | $3.60 |
| Emeril Lagasse 26 qt | $149.99 | $4.50 | $6.75 |
| Chefman 8 qt TurboFry | $79.91 | $2.40 | $3.60 |

Three things make even the higher column an upper bound.

**Commission is paid on Qualifying Revenue, not on the sticker price.** Amazon defines Qualifying Revenue
as the amount it actually receives, less shipping, gift wrapping, handling, taxes, service charges,
credits, rebates, credit card processing fees and bad debt. For a discounted item that is below the price
shown here, not above it.

**The 24-hour session is short.** A session begins on the link click and ends at the earliest of 24 hours
elapsing, the customer placing an order for a physical product, or the customer clicking through someone
else's tagged link. There is no long tail of attribution to rely on.

**Add-to-cart only extends to 89 days.** If the customer adds to cart but does not order, the order must
be completed no later than 89 days after the initial click, and the item must then be shipped and paid
for within 180 days of purchase.

### Where the money actually is in this set

On these numbers the Emeril Lagasse unit is worth roughly 1.9 times a Chefman conversion at either rate,
because the commission is a percentage of a price that is nearly double. That is why the demand data
matters: if the higher-priced unit converts at even two-thirds the rate of the cheaper ones, it earns
more per hundred clicks. Whether it does is unknowable without conversion data from a connected
Associates account.

### Bounty events are worth more than any of this

The rate table also lists fixed-dollar bounty events, and several dwarf the per-unit commission. A Prime
free trial or paid membership pays $12.00. An Audible Premium Plus annual membership pays $25.00 and an
Audible Standard monthly membership pays $20.00. A single Prime signup is worth roughly three to five air
fryer conversions. Any content plan built only around the product commission is leaving the larger line
item on the table.

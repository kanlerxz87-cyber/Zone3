> Products that you have listed on your Site may change, your Site may only show prices and availability
> if: (a) we serve the link in which that price and availability data are displayed, or (b) you obtain
> Product pricing and availability data via Creators API or PA API and you comply with the requirements
> regarding use of Creators API or PA API in the License.

> For example, you must include your Associates ID or "tag" (appearing as XXXXX-##, or such other format
> as we may designate) as a parameter in the URL of each link you place on your Site to an Amazon Site.

Status on this site: **not satisfied**. No tag exists. See F07.

### Redirecting links and link shortening

Amazon, *Commission Income Statement*, section 2(e), disqualifies any purchase made through a
*Redirecting Link*, meaning a link that sends users indirectly to an Amazon Site via an intermediate site
without requiring the customer to click or take some other affirmative action. Amazon,
*Participation Requirements* section 6(w), separately prohibits using a link shortening service in a
manner that makes it unclear you are linking to an Amazon Site.

Status on this site: **resolved by avoidance**. The site publishes canonical `/dp/<ASIN>` URLs, not the
supplied short links. See F03.

### Promotion references must be removed when the promotion ends

Amazon, *Associates Program Participation Requirements*, section 2(b):

> You must remove from your Site any links and related references to limited time promotions as soon as
> that promotion on the relevant Amazon Site ends.

Status on this site: **at risk**. All three products carried a promotion badge at capture. See F08.

### Price tracking and price alerting

Status on this site: **no such feature shipped**. Recorded as F09 because it constrains the pricing

### Original content

Amazon, *Associates Program Participation Requirements*, section 1: your site must contain original
content and be publicly available, and original content using third-party materials must contain
significant commentary, analysis, or transformation.

Status on this site: **satisfied**. All analysis and guide text is original commentary on cited data.

### No incentivising clicks

Amazon, *Participation Requirements* section 6(g): you may not offer any person or entity any
consideration, reward or incentive for using Special Links. Section 6(s) prohibits artificially
generating clicks or impressions.

Status on this site: **satisfied**. Nothing on the site rewards a click.

### Amazon's site must not be framed or cloaked

Amazon, *Participation Requirements* sections 6(n) and 6(v): you may not frame an Amazon Site, and you may
not cloak, hide, spoof or otherwise obscure the URL of your site containing Special Links.

Status on this site: **satisfied**. Links are plain outbound anchors.

you have complied with the Agreement. Failure to do so is a material breach.

Status on this site: **noted**. Keep records of where each value came from; this site's data layer and

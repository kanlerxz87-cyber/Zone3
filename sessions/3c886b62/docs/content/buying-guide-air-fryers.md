## How to choose between these three air fryers

This guide only uses values verified on Amazon for these three products. Where a number could not be
confirmed, it says so.

### Start with capacity, because it is the one hard difference

The three units sit in genuinely different size classes. The CHEFMAN dual basket is 6 quarts total, split
across two independent 3 quart baskets. The Chefman TurboFry is a single 8 quart basket. The Emeril
Lagasse is 26 quarts and is built as a French door toaster oven with a rotisserie, not as a basket
fryer at all. Two independent baskets let you finish two foods at different times; a single large basket
lets you cook one large batch; a 26 quart oven is a countertop replacement for more than a fryer.

### Then decide what else you want the appliance to do

The CHEFMAN dual basket advertises Sync Finish, so the two baskets finish together, plus Hi-Fry and auto
shutoff. The Chefman 8 quart is described as a 4-in-1 with a 450 degrees Fahrenheit Hi-Fry option that
also bakes, dehydrates and handles frozen food. The Emeril Lagasse carries 24 cooking functions with a
dual-speed fan and a rotisserie. Read the function lists as claims from the product titles and feature
lines, not as independently tested results.

### Consider what you are paying

At capture the two Chefman units were both just under $80, at $79.93 and $79.91, while the Emeril Lagasse
was $149.99 against a $209.99 list price. All three were discounted, and all three had cheaper used or
new offers available: $57.45, $49.54 and $92.99 respectively. Prices move, and the values here are a
single capture, so check the current price on Amazon before deciding.

### Ratings do not separate them

The three sit within 0.2 stars of each other: 4.2 out of 5 on 4.8K ratings for the CHEFMAN dual basket,
4.3 out of 5 on 18.3K ratings for the Emeril Lagasse, and 4.4 out of 5 on 16.8K ratings for the Chefman
8 quart. Amazon rounds those counts, so treat them as approximate. On volume, the Emeril Lagasse and the
Chefman 8 quart have far larger review bases and far higher recent purchase counts than the CHEFMAN dual
basket.

### A note on review volume versus rating

A 4.4 on 16.8K ratings is a different evidence base from a 4.2 on 4.8K ratings. The larger sample is
harder to move, so a high rating across many thousands of ratings is a stronger signal than the same
rating across a few thousand. That is the main reason the two higher-volume units read as safer
recommendations than the dual basket, independent of features.

### What this guide cannot tell you

It cannot tell you about durability, noise, cleaning effort, or how any of these compare to air fryers
outside this set of three, because those are not published on an official Amazon page and no other source
was used. It also cannot tell you the energy use, warranty terms, or exact internal dimensions, none of
which were verifiable from the pages cited here.

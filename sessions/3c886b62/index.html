Findings

Findings

One niche, three direct comparators. All three supplied links resolve to air fryers in Home & Kitchen. There is no category sprawl, which makes a single comparison page and a single buying guide the right shape for the content.

The Emeril Lagasse unit leads on every demand signal. It has 18.3K ratings and 4K+ units bought in the past month, against 16.8K and 3K+ for the Chefman 8 quart and 4.8K and 1K+ for the CHEFMAN dual basket. It is also the most expensive at $149.99, so it is the highest-value click if the commission category resolves favourably.

The CHEFMAN dual basket is the weak seller of the three. 1K+ bought in the past month is a third of the Emeril unit's volume, and it has the smallest review base at 4.8K ratings. It also has the lowest star rating of the three at 4.2.

Every product was discounted at capture. Discount depth ranges from 20.1% off list for the CHEFMAN dual basket to 33.4% for the Chefman 8 quart, with the Emeril unit at 28.6%. Two carry a Limited time deal badge and one an Early Black Friday Deal badge, so all three promotion references have an expiry the project does not control.

Every product has cheaper used or new offers. The lowest alternative offer sits below the Amazon price on all three: $57.45 against $79.93, $92.99 against $149.99, and $49.54 against $79.91. A reviewer who promises the lowest price will be wrong.

Ratings are tightly clustered. 4.2, 4.3 and 4.4 out of 5 across all three. Rating is not a differentiator in this set, which means content has to compete on capacity, function count and price rather than on quality claims.

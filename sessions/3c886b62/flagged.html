F01		Displaying static prices conflicts with Associates Program Participation Requirements section 2(b)

Amazon's Participation Requirements section 2(b) states: 'Product prices and availability may vary from time to time. Because prices for and availability of Products that you have listed on your Site may change, your Site may only show prices and availability if: (a) we serve the link in which that price and availability data are displayed, or (b) you obtain Product pricing and availability data via Creators API or PA API and you comply with the requirements regarding use of Creators API or PA API in the License.' The prices on this site were captured by a one-time read of an Amazon page, not served by Amazon and not obtained via PA API / Creators API.

Impact: The price columns cannot go live on an Associates property in their current form without risking a breach of the Operating Agreement.

Action: Either (1) obtain PA API / Creators API credentials and rebuild the price columns from that feed, or (2) remove price values from the public pages and keep them only in the internal analysis documents until such credentials exist.

F03		The supplied link.amazon short links may be 'Redirecting Links' under Commission Income Statement section 2(e)

Commission Income Statement section 2(e) disqualifies 'any Product purchased by a customer who is referred to an Amazon Site by a link that sends users indirectly to an Amazon Site via an intermediate site, without requiring the customer to click on a link or take some other affirmative action on that intermediate site (a "Redirecting Link").' A link.amazon/<code> URL is an intermediate URL that redirects straight through to the product page with no affirmative action by the customer. Separately, Participation Requirements section 6(w) prohibits using 'a link shortening service, button, hyperlink or other ad placement in a manner that makes it unclear that you are linking to an Amazon Site.'

Impact: If the supplied short links are used as the Special Links themselves, resulting purchases are at risk of being disqualified and the commission forfeited.

Action: Tag and publish the resolved canonical /dp/<ASIN> URLs (S1, S3, S5) rather than the link.amazon short links. Keep the short links only as the provenance record of what was supplied.

F04		The three short-link codes are 9 characters, so they are not Amazon ASINs

B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3 and B00hJ57DV are each 9 characters. A standard Amazon ASIN is 10 characters. They also contain mixed-case letters, which ASINs do not. They are opaque short-link slugs that happen to begin with 'B0'.

Impact: Anyone reading the brief could mistake the codes for ASINs and build the wrong product set. This site resolves them to the real ASINs B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R and B08DKYBTPH.

Action: Confirm with the link owner that the three resolved products are the intended ones.

The links resolve today to live amazon.com product pages (verified). However, link.amazon is not among the Amazon short-link domains Amazon documents for public use; Amazon's commonly documented shortener is amzn.to.

## Decisions before commissioning another story

- **Does each link credit this account?** Unknown. Five matching listing titles don't establish who receives credit. Keep [FLG-003](../../flagged.html#FLG-003) open and preserve the blank intent fields. The decision requires a dated enrolled-account destination and report check, not a purchase by the publisher. - **Can the site say that one format converts better?** No authorized orders or eligible sessions are available. The collector accepts five aggregate events and respects browser privacy signals. Preview counts test delivery; they do not belong in the campaign ledger. [GitHub describes Pages as static hosting](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages); a separate production host with a verified retention and logging configuration is needed.
- **What should go on the next cover?** Treat “What fits in one layer?” as an editorial proposal, not a measured trend. At the scheduled review, compare dated query impressions and clicks for capacity versus cleanup, keeping the reporting period and page constant. Without an authorized query export, keep both candidates on the [decision desk](../../suggestions/index.html), not in an automated publishing queue.
- **Which visual change is worth making next?** Ask a reader to find the oven format and then the handheld thermometer on a phone without coaching. Record where they hesitate before changing the category names. Design prompt: show the meal on the cover, show the format on the card, and put the one question that distinguishes it immediately before the listing link. Do not borrow review stars, invented bylines or supplier photography to create authority.

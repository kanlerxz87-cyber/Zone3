One of these three is on Amazon's best-seller list today

Not the one most people would guess. What the list is, what a rank means on the day you read it, and why we publish the date.

2026-11-16 · 5 min read · The Crisp Report

Most 'best air fryers of 2026' pages are a stack of affiliate links wearing a headline. Ours would be, too, if we had a reason to rank these three. We don't. What we do have is Amazon's own list, read today.

On 2026-11-16 the Emeril Lagasse 26QT appeared at position #6 on Amazon's Best Sellers in Air Fryers page, which Amazon itself describes as its most popular products based on sales, updated frequently S63. The other two were not in the first six positions in the page we read.

What that rank is, and is not

  • It is a sales ranking in one browse node. Air Fryers is node 17659096011, sitting under Fryers (17659095011), under Small Appliances (289913) S64. Two of our three products live in that node according to the breadcrumb on their own listings.
  • It is not a review. No stars, no editorial judgement, no durability data. A best-seller list measures what people bought, which correlates with price and availability as much as with cooking.
  • It is not stable. Amazon says the list is updated frequently. A rank quoted without a date is a screenshot of a river.
  • It is not the whole niche. Air Fryer Ovens (224479505011) is a sibling node with its own list; a machine can be an oven in your kitchen and an air fryer in the catalogue, and the boundary moves between departments S64.

The same list, one week apart, can disagree with itself

We have history here. Two earlier captures of this category page, on 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14, ordered two of the positions differently from each other. That is recorded in this site's own flag register as FLG-025 rather than smoothed over.

The same drift shows up in product titles. One earlier capture of the Emeril Lagasse 26QT listing rendered its title with the words 24 cooking functions, dual-speed fan and rotisserie S53; the title we were served on 2026-11-16 says 24 One-Touch Preset Options, Extra Large Interior, Six Cooking Accessories Included S61. Same ASIN, same destination, different sentence. Manufacturers edit listing copy.

Which is the honest way to use a rank: it is a data point for the day it was read, on the page that was read, from the account that read it. That is also why every trend sentence on this site carries a date and a link.

A rank without a date is a rumour with a screenshot.

What we will not do with this

We will not call the Emeril Lagasse 26QT the best of the three, or 'trending', or 'loved by thousands'. Being sixth in a category that updates constantly is not a quality claim, and Amazon's own Program Policies bar a site from making inaccurate or misleading claims about a product S02. Neither we nor you can decide that a position on a list is a reason to buy.

What you can take from it: in a niche where a buyer's first instinct is to grab the biggest number, the sixth-most-popular item in the Air Fryers node today is a 26QT oven, not a basket. That is a fact about the market, and a decent prompt for your own decision.

What this article cannot answer

  • Sales figures behind the ranking are not published on the list page, so no number can be quoted for them S63.
  • Prices of the top positions were visible in the capture and are deliberately not published: Program Policies 2(b) permits price display only when Amazon serves the price data or it comes through the Creators API S02 S63.
  • Star ratings and review counts appear on the same page and are likewise withheld under Program Policies 6(t) S02.

Unanswered items are also on the flagged register. A blank we left on purpose is better than a number we made up.

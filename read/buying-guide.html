The five questions

Buying an air fryer in 2026: five questions before you spend

A short guide that starts with your kitchen and ends with the listing, because in that order the marketing gets a lot easier to see through.

2026-11-16 · The Crisp Report

1

How many things do you cook at the same time?

This is the fork in the road, and it is answered by your dinner, not by a spec sheet. Two foods, two settings, one finish: divided baskets — the Chefman 6 Qt dual basket is the only one of our three built that way S14. One big batch: a single XL basket, like the Chefman TurboFry 8 Qt S16. Whole dish, sheet pan or a roast: an oven cavity, like the Emeril Lagasse 26QT S15.

2

What can you permanently give up on the counter?

An air fryer that lives under a cabinet is an air fryer you will stop using. Measure the space you would let it occupy forever, then check clearance for the door: a French door swings sideways and needs room to open, which the Emeril Lagasse 26QT's own format implies but no title states S15. No external dimensions appear in any of the three titles we captured, so this is a tape-measure job and then a listing check S14 S15 S16.

3

Which modes will you actually press?

Bake, dehydrate, frozen, rotisserie, toast, proof, sterilise — the counts in this category climb endlessly. Read them as a list of presets, not capabilities. Then notice what is stated as a number, because that is the only part you can verify: Chefman TurboFry 8 Qt gives its hot mode a ceiling of 450°F, while Chefman 6 Qt dual basket names the same style of mode with no temperature S14 S16.

4

Who washes it?

Ask which parts are dishwasher-safe, because the listing answers that question and only that one: baskets and baskets-plus, on two of our three S14 S16. The Emeril Lagasse 26QT title states accessories instead, and says nothing about washing S15. If a twice-a-week wipe-down of a cavity is unacceptable to you, that absence is your answer, and it is worth reading the full listing over.

5

What do you need to know that the listing will not say?

Write the two or three questions down before you buy — wattage, rack positions, whether the twin baskets merge, whether the coating is PFAS-free — and go look. A buying decision that survives its own open questions is a decision you will not revisit. This site's own unresolved items are published on the method page, because a guide that hides its gaps is a brochure.

Four things not to do

Do not shop by quart number alone

Two 3Qt drawers and one 8Qt drawer are different objects that happen to share a unit. Divide first, compare second.

Do not treat a best-seller rank as a review

Amazon's list is a sales ranking that updates frequently. Ours quotes it with a date attached and stops there S63.

Do not assume a price you saw last week

Prices move, and by policy this site may not print them anyway. The button on every product card here goes to the official listing where the live number is S02.

Do not let an adjective do a spec's job

'Crispier results' is a comparison with the second term missing. Numbers are checkable; adjectives are not S16.

Questions people ask us

Are these three products tested by you?

No. Nothing on this site claims first-hand use, because the FTC's position is that an endorser cannot describe experience they did not have. We read the official pages and did the arithmetic S07 S24.

Why is there no price on the product cards?

Amazon's Program Policies permit price and availability display only where Amazon serves that data, or via the Creators API. No API access here, so no price here — a stale number would be worse than none S02.

Why no star ratings?

Same rulebook, different clause: reviews and star ratings may be shown only when obtained through the Creators API or PA API. The older API is deprecated with HTTP 403 responses S02 S05.

Is Kitchen really a 4.50% category?

That is the rate printed on Amazon's published Standard Commission Income Statement Table 1 for the row 'Physical Books, Kitchen, Automotive'. Account-specific schedules can differ and are not public S25.

Which of the three should I buy?

That depends on questions 1 and 2 above, which only you can answer. The comparison page is built so that you can decide in about four minutes without us deciding for you.

How this site gets paid, and what that does to the copy

The commission rate, the click window, the payment lag and the rules about which numbers we are even allowed to print. No vague 'we may earn a commission'. Actual terms.

2026-11-16 · 5 min read · The Crisp Report

The FTC's line on affiliate content is short: disclose the relationship clearly and conspicuously, close to the recommendation, because readers cannot be expected to know that an 'affiliate link' or a 'buy now' button means somebody gets paid S44 S07. So here is the whole arrangement, in numbers.

Every figure below was read from an official Amazon Associates page on 2026-11-16. Nothing here is a rate card from memory.

The rate

Two honest caveats. First, this is the standard published table; an individual account can be on different terms, and the account-level schedule sits behind sign-in, so we cannot quote a rate for this site's own account. Second, we are not assigning a category to any of the three products beyond what the breadcrumb says, and therefore we are not stating an earning per product. Kitchen is the row that a reader will infer. It is published as a category reference only.

Bounties are a separate line: $12.00 for a successful Prime free-trial or paid membership registration, $2.00 for a Prime Video free trial, $5.00 for a first stream S65.

The clock

  • 24 hours. A session starts when someone clicks the link, and it ends when 24 hours have elapsed S02.
  • 89 days, with a catch. A purchase completed from the cart counts if it happens no later than 89 days after that initial click S02.
  • 180 days to be real. Within 180 days of the purchase the item must be shipped or downloaded and paid for S02.
  • Returns disqualify. An order with a cancellation, return or refund initiated is not a qualifying purchase S02.

The money, and the delay

Commission income is paid about 60 days after the end of the calendar month in which it was earned S02. US payment minimums published in the same statement are 10 USD for a deposit, 10 USD for a gift card, 100 USD for a check, with a 15 USD check processing fee S02.

There is also a gate before the account earns anything at all. Amazon's help pages state that an application is reviewed once you have driven at least three qualifying sales in the first 180 days, that personal orders do not count, and that an account can be closed for not referring a sale within 180 days S29 S28.

Which is the useful way to read this page: an affiliate site is not a publisher with a side income. It is a small business with a 60-day lag, a three-sale audition and a rulebook. We would rather you saw the machinery than a lifestyle.

What the rules stop us from telling you

This is the part that makes our pages look like everyone else's, minus the numbers. Amazon's Program Policies say a site may show prices and availability only when Amazon serves that data in the link, or when the site pulls it from the Creators API S02. Star ratings and customer reviews may be shown only if obtained through the Creators API or PA API S02.

We have no API credentials. The API itself has an entry requirement — at least ten qualifying sales in the previous 30 days, alongside enrolment and registration S04 — and the older Product Advertising API is retired, returning HTTP 403 S05.

So a price on this page would be a screenshot from Tuesday. Instead: no prices, no stars, no review counts, and a link on every product card that takes you to the official listing where the current number actually lives. That is the whole explanation.

We could paste a price here. It would be wrong within a day. So we paste a link instead.

What this article cannot answer

  • The rate this site's own account would be paid is not verifiable from public pages; the account-level schedule is behind sign-in S03.
  • No Associates tag has been supplied for this project, so no link on this site is currently a qualifying Special Link, and no commission can be earned until one is S02. That is the single biggest open item and it is listed on the site's flagged register.

Unanswered items are also on the flagged register. A blank we left on purpose is better than a number we made up.

Four rules this site will not break

We quote, we don't guess

Every number on this site comes from the text of the listing Amazon served us, or from Amazon's own programme pages. Capacity, preset counts and accessories are quoted, never rounded up or estimated.

No prices, no stars — on purpose

Amazon's Program Policies allow a site to show prices and availability only when Amazon serves them, and star ratings only when they come through the Creators API. We have neither, so we publish neither rather than something stale. S02

We have not cooked with these

No product was tested for this site. The FTC's guidance is that you cannot claim an experience you did not have, so you will get analysis instead of fake enthusiasm. S07 S24

Unknowns stay visible

Where the listing does not answer something, the article says so in the open. The running list of unanswered questions lives on the method page and the site's flagged register. S01

Executive Summary

What this platform is, what was verified, and what remains open.

Purpose

Zone3 collects, organizes, and analyzes Amazon affiliate products end to end: niche identification, product research, pricing/review/competition/commission analysis, SEO-ready article outlines, affiliate-link management, click/conversion tracking, and campaign optimization — prioritizing automation, scalability, data-driven decisions, and Amazon Associates compliance (disclosure requirements).

Products in scope

Three Amazon affiliate links were researched. All three resolve (via Amazon's own link.amazon short links) to real Amazon.com product pages in Home & Kitchen → Kitchen, i.e. air fryers / countertop cooking appliances.

  • CHEFMAN 6 Quart Dual Basket Air Fryer Oven — B0CDY277D3 (from link.amazon/B04G1MEwY)
  • Emeril Lagasse 26QT French Door Multi-Function Air Fryer Toaster Oven — B09B7SB46R (from link.amazon/B02NbDxp3)
  • Chefman Air Fryer 8 Qt TurboFry 4-in-1 — B08DKYBTPH (from link.amazon/B00hJ57DV)

Key verified findings

  • All three products are in Amazon's Kitchen commission category → 4.50% standard commission rate (US Associates Program, official Commission Income Statement).
  • Every table row links to an official Amazon source. Nothing was inferred or invented.
  • Amazon served a bot-protection checkpoint on product detail pages, so price, star rating, review count, and availability are unverified — left blank and listed on the Flagged for Review page.
  • Short-link ASINs (B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV) do not exist as standalone Amazon pages (404 across six marketplaces); only the short links resolve to the destination ASINs above. Flagged for review.

Compliance

Amazon Associates Operating Agreement §5 requires a clear and prominent disclosure on every page: "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." See Affiliate Disclosure and Flagged for Review.

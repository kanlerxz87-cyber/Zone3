Admission rule

A source is admitted if Amazon or the FTC publishes it themselves at a first-party domain and this session could fetch it. Web-search results were used only to locate candidate official pages; the substance of every row above comes from the fetched primary document, and the search snippets were not treated as evidence.

Two entries are recorded as deliberately negative: SRC-12 (the rate card, which redirects to sign-in) and SRC-01…03/04…06 (the direct probes and observed short-link destinations). A retrieval observation is exactly what makes the corresponding blanks on the site defensible rather than merely empty.

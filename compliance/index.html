Document versions

Associates Program Operating Agreement — updated October 15, 2025 — affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement

Associates Program Policies — updated April 14, 2026 — affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies

Both fetched in full on 2026-11-13. Amazon revises these documents without notice and links a "what's changed" comparison page, so re-verification is a scheduled step (Methodology) rather than a one-off. The Program Policies are incorporated by reference into the Agreement, and breach of the Participation Requirements or IP License is expressly deemed material breach.

Practical note on enforcement teeth. Amazon states it may permanently cease payment of all commission income, whether or not related to the violation, without notice — so the downside of a single careless automation is the whole balance, not the offending transaction. That asymmetry is the reason compliance sits in the build pipeline here rather than in a style guide.

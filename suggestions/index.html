THE NEXT DESIGN BRIEF

A prompt worth building from.

This is a proposed design direction grounded in the limits above—not an instruction to treat missing evidence as confirmed.

Design Zone3 Kitchen as a warm, restrained kitchen publication, not an analytics dashboard. Lead with real buyer questions and a source-linked five-product shortlist: three air fryers and two meat thermometers. Use an ivory background, deep green typography, editorial serif headlines, generous spacing, purposeful diagrammatic art and accessible mobile navigation. Keep the Associates disclosure immediately below the masthead and repeated in the footer. Make every outbound Amazon product link sponsored and clearly label the Amazon destination. Keep all research dashboards behind one navigation group and accessible in the footer. Separate intended ASINs and confirmation fields from observed redirects. Never invent a reviewer, test result, price, rating, certification, popularity signal, order, conversion score or revenue forecast. Use labelled generic art until authorized exact-product assets exist. Build useful question-led guides with honest authorship, source dates, related links, canonical URLs, sitemap and RSS coverage. Preserve the single five-event privacy counter and require a durable collector before production activation. Keep the campaign ledger empty until authorized first-party evidence arrives. Suggestions must expose known facts, missing information, discovery steps, safe defaults and the evidence needed for a decision. Saving a review must not implement it. Publication, API content and account-level changes stay gated on authorization and evidence.

The publishing gate.

  1. DiscoverUse authorized first-party inputs. Keep provenance and capture date.
  2. DraftQuestion-led, source-linked copy. Missing facts stay absent.
  3. CheckDisclosures, sponsored links, intent, API permissions, unsupported claims, feed and sitemap.
  4. ApproveRequire real evidence and account authority for gated capabilities.
  5. MeasureUse real query, click and report evidence—not forecasts—to choose the next question.

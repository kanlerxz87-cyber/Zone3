How the disclosure is placed

The wording is the Operating Agreement Section 5 sentence, unchanged: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. The sentence sits directly under the masthead of each page, so a reader sees it before the first product. The footer repeats it. The footer is an addition, not a substitute, because the FTC says disclosures “are likely to be missed if they appear only on an ABOUT ME or profile page, at the end of posts or videos, or anywhere that requires a person to click MORE” (the page applies the same placement test) (FTC Disclosures 101).

Suggestions & decisions

Design and marketing proposals for the affiliate site, the open questions that must be answered before any decision, and how to discover the missing information. Proposals are labelled as proposals; every fact carries its source.

What this page is. Design and marketing proposals for making this site read like a polished marketing affiliate website — catchy, topic-driven articles, honest trend sections and conversion-ready pages — together with the questions that must be answered, and the ways to discover the missing information, before any proposal is implemented. Every statement of fact below links the official page it was read from; every proposal is labelled Proposal and is not a verified fact. Nothing on this page is implemented by this session: it exists so a reviewer can choose the best path first.

Design and marketing suggestions

IDSuggestionWhat it isWhy it helps / complianceStatus
SUG-01Brand and hero treatment for the landing page
Give the site a marketing name, a one-line promise and a hero section that leads with the three air-fryer products and what a reader gets (comparisons, buying guides, verified specs). Use Amazon-provided Program Content (images, link formats) served through the link, not scraped screenshots.
A polished marketing site starts with one clear promise above the fold. Amazon's Operating Agreement makes Program Content (data, images, text, link formats, widgets) available to Associates, so official assets can be used without inventing product photography.
  • Program Content is made available for advertising items; it excludes product data from non-Amazon sites. S01
Proposal
SUG-02Editorial article hub built from the verified topic plan
Publish the 12 working article titles already planned on the Content topics page as real articles: a hub listing them with working titles, the verified Amazon fact each rests on, and a short summary. Give each article a byline, a date and a 'what we verified / what we did not' note.
Catchy, topic-driven articles are the main organic-traffic lever for an affiliate site, and the topic plan already anchors every title to a fact printed on the listing, so the content has something real to say instead of generic filler.
  • No topic may state a price, star rating or review count unless Amazon serves the link or the data comes from the API. S02
  • No topic may claim search volume, trend direction or conversion data: Amazon publishes no such affiliate endpoint. S02
Proposal
SUG-03Human-review gate to remove the AI-slop feel
Before publication, every article passes a review checklist: specific verified specs, no generic filler, no invented experience, the disclosure shown near the recommendation, and a named reviewer. A short 'how this was written' note states which facts were read from which Amazon page.
The brief asks that the site not read like AI slop. The most defensible way to achieve that without inventing claims is a visible editorial process: every statement traces to an Amazon page, and every article says what was and was not verified.
  • FTC: you cannot talk about your experience with a product you have not tried. S07
  • FTC: the disclosure must be hard to miss and placed close to the recommendation; an 'affiliate link' label or a buy button alone is not an adequate disclosure. S44
Proposal
SUG-04Comparison and buying-guide pages in marketing style
Restyle the existing comparison and buying-guide pages (marketing/comparison.html, marketing/buying-guide.html) with a clear comparison matrix and a 'who this is for' buying guide, using only verified listing attributes (capacity, features, brand) from the destination page titles.
Comparisons and buying guides are the highest-intent content for air fryer buyers and convert readers into clicks; a clean matrix plus a decision guide reads like a real marketing site rather than a data dump.
  • Prices, availability, ratings and review counts may appear only when Amazon serves the link or the data comes from the Creators API or PA API. S02
Proposal
SUG-05Conversion-oriented calls to action with tagged Special Links
Add clear 'Check price on Amazon' style calls to action that point to Amazon through the owner's tagged Special Links, with the disclosure adjacent to each CTA.
Blocked: The Associates tag is not known to this session (FLG-010). Implementation waits on owner input Q1.
Marketing sites convert through clear next steps. This depends entirely on the owner supplying the Associates tag; without it no link earns commission.
  • Every Special Link must carry the Associates tag (Participation Requirements 2(b)). S02
  • FTC: a 'buy now' button alone is not an adequate disclosure. S44
Blocked
SUG-06Trust section: how the site stays accurate
Add a trust section explaining the verification protocol: every value comes from an official Amazon or FTC page, unverified cells stay blank, and the Flagged for review page publishes open items with their source links.
Affiliate trust is a conversion factor. The site already has a verification discipline; presenting it plainly distinguishes it from sloppy AI sites and answers the credibility question before a reader clicks.
  • Operating Agreement Section 5 disclosure wording must remain prominent wherever Program Content is used. S01
Proposal
SUG-07SEO mechanics with honest meta descriptions
Keep the generated meta descriptions, canonical links and sitemap, and add article-level metadata when articles are published. Meta descriptions state what the page is and never claim metrics that cannot be sourced.
Clean metadata and a current sitemap are the baseline for organic traffic and are already generated by the build; extending them to articles costs little and keeps every claim traceable.
  • No search-volume, trend or conversion claims are made because no official source publishes them for affiliates. S02
Proposal
SUG-08Trending-topics section anchored to point-in-time official surfaces
Add an editorial 'in the kitchen now' style section that points to what the listings themselves show (verified attributes and the Amazon Best Sellers page as a point-in-time reference), rather than asserting trends.
The brief asks for trendy topics. A trend claim with no source is exactly the AI-slop the brief wants avoided; anchoring to a named, dated official surface keeps the section honest and still feels current.
  • Amazon's category Best Sellers page is the only official popularity surface; it is point-in-time and moves between captures. S43
  • No search-volume or trend-direction claims may be made. S02
Proposal
SUG-09Click and conversion tracking through Amazon's own reporting
Track clicks and conversions using the reporting Amazon provides to Associates, and record campaign performance in the Campaigns ledger only from those sources. Do not add third-party link-tracking that could create artificial sessions.
The brief requires click/conversion tracking and continuous optimization. Amazon's own reporting is the compliant data path; it also feeds the ledger with values that can be sourced rather than guessed.
  • Participation Requirements prohibit artificial clicks or sessions. S02
Proposal
SUG-10Content cadence and a named editor before scaling articles
Agree a publishing cadence and a named reviewer for the article hub before scaling it, and record each decision on the coordination board so implementation is traceable.
Scaling content without a review gate is how sites acquire the AI-slop reputation the brief wants avoided; a cadence plus named owner makes the editorial process real rather than cosmetic.
  • FTC guidance on experience claims applies to any first-person content. S07

How to use this page

  1. 1. Review this page with the suggestions (SUG-01 to SUG-10) and the open questions (Q1 to Q10) side by side.
  2. 2. Answer each open question from the owner; record the answer and its source or note that it is a decision, not a fact.
  3. 3. For each suggestion, choose Accept, Reject or Defer, and record the choice and the reason in COORDINATION.md under this session's part.
  4. 4. Only accepted suggestions are implemented, in a follow-up session that re-verifies the affected pages against official sources before publishing.
  5. 5. Blocked suggestions (currently SUG-05) stay blocked until the named blocker is resolved; nothing on this page is implemented by this session.

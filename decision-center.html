What we know, what is missing, and how to discover it

Answers below are limited to the repository's sourced record and official program documents. “Unknown” means the current evidence does not settle it; it is not a guess.

1 · Which five products were intended?

Known: The shop register records five submitted links, five observed Amazon destinations and blank intended-ASIN fields. The Creators API reference defines ASIN identifiers as ten characters, but a token-shape comparison does not determine whether a supplied short link is valid or compliant. S31 · S45

Missing: Owner-confirmed intended listing / ASIN mapping for all five links. Redirect observations do not establish intent. Shop intent record

Discovery: Ask the requester to confirm each intended product and official listing, then record its ASIN/URL/date/source separately from the observed redirect before making product-specific claims.

2 · Can the platform fetch product content automatically?

Known: Creators API documentation lists eligibility prerequisites, including at least 10 qualifying sales in the prior 30 days. The PA-API 5 deprecation notice and Creators API introduction are in the source register. S04 · S05

Missing: Associates enrollment, qualifying-sales status, API access approval, marketplace, credentials and an authorized data-use path. FLG-006

Discovery: Check Associates Central eligibility and API access; use a secrets manager for credentials. Never place keys in this public static repository. The answer is not assumed by this build.

3 · Which facts may appear beside affiliate links?

Known: Amazon policy sets conditions for displaying price/availability and customer ratings/reviews; see sections 2(b) and 6(t) in the policies. S02

Missing: An approved data route for each live product and a verified match between each fact and the intended ASIN. Pricing · Reviews

Discovery: Keep volatile price, rating, review count and rank fields blank until the exact product and policy-permitted source path are verified. Do not convert old screenshots or snapshots into live offers.

4 · Are the submitted links suitable for affiliate attribution?

Known: Amazon policy describes Special Link and attribution requirements. S02

Missing: Whether each submitted link meets the applicable requirements, whether shopnow047c-20 belongs to the enrolled account/marketplace, and whether the resulting Special Links receive report attribution. FLG-003

Discovery: After confirming intended products, generate and test one Associates Special Link per product in the enrolled workflow; verify each destination and report before scaling. The supplied short links remain unresolved, not declared invalid.

5 · What commission and revenue can be forecast?

Known: Amazon publishes standard and onsite category tables, while the account schedule is a separate source. The current record does not assign a per-product rate. S03 · S25 · S26

Missing: Confirmed product category, account-specific rate, eligible sessions, qualifying orders, cancellations and actual commission reports. FLG-005 · Campaign ledger

Discovery: Obtain the rate applicable to the intended ASIN and a date-bounded performance report. Do not fabricate a “potential earnings” number from category averages.

6 · Which niche or article is actually trending?

Known: No verified trend or search-volume evidence is included in this project's approved inputs. An article title is an editorial hypothesis, not demand proof.

Missing: Audience geography, first-party queries, search impressions/clicks, seasonality window, competitor set and conversion performance.

Discovery: Connect the publisher's own search-performance export and Associates reports, date and preserve each measurement, then rank topics against observed demand. No score is assigned while inputs are absent.

7 · Which marketplace and disclosure apply?

Known: All five dated observations point to Amazon.com pages. That does not establish the target country, reader audience or Associates marketplace. The Associates disclosure statement and FTC endorsement resources are in the cited register. S01 · S07 · S24

Missing: Owner confirmation of audience and enrolled marketplace(s), plus any applicable channel-specific disclosure review.

Discovery: Confirm audience and marketplace in the owner's account before localizing links. Keep the Associate disclosure clear and proximate to recommendations; do not infer target market from observed destinations or the requester's location.

8 · What can safely publish before inputs arrive?

Known: Source-backed buying education can explain comparison criteria without asserting unsupported product performance. Amazon policy and FTC resources remain the relevant compliance anchors. S02 S01 · S07 · S24

Missing: First-party audience evidence to validate which topic deserves priority.

Discovery: Publish an evergreen checklist only after editorial review; record impressions and clicks once a compliant measurement route exists. Product-specific price/rating copy stays out until the evidence gate passes.

Suggestions to review before implementation

Priority is based on resolving blockers and reducing unsupported claims, not on assumed conversion lift. Editorial topic labels below are not verified trend rankings.

Priority 1 · unblock identity

Build a product-intent confirmation record

For each submitted link, store the intended ASIN, official listing URL, confirmation date, and source. Keep observed redirect targets in a separate field. Do not merge the two fields. Rationale: Amazon's ASIN definition provides a checkable identifier format. S31 · S45

Priority 2 · attribution safety

Replace ambiguous outbound links with validated Special Links

Test one link per product in the enrolled Associates workflow and confirm destination and tracking report before scaling. Do not claim that the supplied short links are invalid; their exact compliance status is unresolved in FLG-003. S02

Priority 3 · editorial value

Lead with useful, human-sounding buyer questions

Suggested editorial hypotheses: “Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?”, “A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity”, “How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs”, and “Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance.” These are working titles—not claims about popularity, search volume or product superiority.

Priority 4 · evidence-based iteration

Measure before automating optimization

Start with an empty but well-defined campaign ledger; add timestamp, source, channel, eligible sessions, clicks and reported orders only when obtained from authorized first-party reports. Keep a dated provenance trail. No conversion score or revenue forecast is presented here.

Priority 5 · compliance gate

Put disclosures and API policy checks in the publishing workflow

Keep the Associates statement visible and review disclosure placement and endorsement context against the Operating Agreement and FTC guidance. S01 · S07 · S24

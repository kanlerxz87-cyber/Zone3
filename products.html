How to read this table

  • Each row is one product link from the brief. An identifier is counted only when a product page under that identifier exists and matches it.
  • “Observed redirect” is the page the short link opened on its observation date (products 1–3: 2026-11-13 and re-checked 2026-11-16; products 4–5, added to the brief on 2026-11-17: fetched twice that day, S70–S75). It is not treated as the submitted product, because the submitted identifier does not resolve to it; the intended product is a separate field that stays empty until the owner confirms it — see the five-link intake register.
  • The product-intent confirmation record on this page is where an owner confirmation is stored: intended ASIN, official listing URL, confirmation date and source, one block per submitted link. Every cell is blank until that confirmation arrives, and the observed redirect is never copied into it — an observation is not evidence of intent (FLG-002, 0C4-F-02).
  • Product name, brand, price, rating and availability are blank because they cannot be confirmed (see Pricing and Reviews).
  • Products 4 and 5 open meat-thermometer listings in a different Amazon category from the site's air-fryer coverage; the scope question is open (CBB-F-03).

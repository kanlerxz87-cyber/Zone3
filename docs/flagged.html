F1 — Supplied codes are 9-character short-link paths, not ASINs needs review

What we observed: the three supplied path codes are each 9 characters:

Supplied codeLengthValid ASIN lengthDirect amazon.com/dp/<code>
B04G1MEwY910HTTP 404 — "Page Not Found" (verify)
B02NbDxp3910HTTP 404 — "Page Not Found" (verify)

Why it matters: Amazon defines an ASIN as a 10-character code (e.g., B07FZ8S74R); the codes as supplied cannot be ASINs and cannot be looked up directly on Amazon. They are link.amazon short-link path codes.

What we did: followed each short link through to its real destination ASIN and verified the products there (see Products). The destination ASINs are valid 10-character codes.

Sources: 404 test #1 · 404 test #2 · 404 test #3; ASIN format per Amazon Seller Central — ASIN and multiple Amazon-help references (see Sources).

Suggested action: confirm with the link provider that the intended identifiers are the destination ASINs (B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH), or obtain full amzn.to//?tag= links.

What we observed: link.amazon/<code> redirects via amzlinks.in (a third-party short-link operator) before landing on amazon.com. Also, an Associates tag parameter could not be verified in the redirect chain we observed — meaning we cannot confirm from an official source that these links carry a monetizable Associates ID.

Suggested action: replace/augment with links generated from your own Associates account (SiteStripe or ?tag=YOUR-ID-20) and re-test attribution before driving traffic.

F3 — Commission rates unverifiable (sign-in only) → left blank needs review

What we attempted: the official Associates Commission rate schedule at affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/schedule redirects to sign-in (HTTP redirect to amazon.com/ap/signin). The Policies page's Appendix links to that schedule but does not publish the rates inline.

Result: no category commission percentage for Home & Kitchen / Kitchen could be confirmed from an official, publicly accessible source.

What we did instead: the commission-rate and estimated-commission cells in Products and Analysis §4 are blank and marked unverified. We did not use third-party rate cards, cached figures, or estimates.

Sources: rate schedule (sign-in required) · Policies → Commission Income Statement §3 + Appendix (both fetched 2026-11-13).

Suggested action: sign in to Associates Central and paste the official rate for the applicable category; then fill the blank cells and remove this flag.

F4 — Best Sellers Rank unverifiable (bot-checkpoint) → left blank needs review

What we observed: Amazon product detail pages intermittently served automated-traffic checkpoints ("To continue, please click the 'Continue shopping' button") to our fetcher, so the Product information section (where BSR is published) could not be read reliably.

Result: BSR cells are blank and marked unverified for all three products. No BSR value, rank direction, or category-rank claim appears anywhere on this site.

What we could still verify: titles, brands, prices, list prices, ratings, review counts and "bought in past month" from Amazon's search-result pages (each row links its source in Products).

Suggested action: re-check BSR manually on each product page (Product information section) or via an approved PA API/Creators API feed, then fill the cells and remove this flag.

F5 — Prices are volatile deal prices (observed 2026-11-13) needs review

What we observed: all three products were on promotion at capture:

Why it matters: (a) prices change frequently — any content quoting them must be re-verified; (b) Associates Program Policies restrict when a site may display prices: only when Amazon serves the link containing the price data, or when data comes from Creators API/PA API (Participation Requirements §2(b)); (c) references to limited-time promotions must be removed as soon as the promotion ends (same section).

What we did: every price cell carries its source link and capture date; the content pipeline includes a mandatory promo-expiry cleanup stage; prices on this site are labeled as research observations, not live quotes.

Suggested action: before commercial launch, switch price display to Amazon-served links/widgets or Creators/PA API data, and schedule daily/weekly re-verification.

Purpose

Zone3 is an Amazon affiliate marketing research site that collects, organizes and analyzes a supplied product list, publishes sourced findings as a static dashboard, and documents compliance with the Amazon Associates Program and FTC disclosure rules. This page summarizes what was done, what the data shows, and what remains open.

Scope delivered

  • 3 supplied short links resolved end-to-end to live Amazon.com listings (each stable across repeated fetches) and recorded with per-row official source links.
  • 45 tracked data cells (15 fields × 3 products): 36 verified from official Amazon pages, 9 left blank and flagged (BSR, commission %, commission $ — no inference).
  • Analysis of pricing, review strength, positioning and niche momentum, each table carrying its source (Analysis).
  • Marketing content kit: product spotlights, a comparison table and a buying guide built strictly on verified facts (SEO Content).
  • Compliance implementation: the exact Associates Operating Agreement §5 disclosure, FTC-aligned placement, and program-rule references (Compliance).
  • Governance: a public Flagged for Review register and a line-by-line Sources & Verification log.

Key findings

  1. One coherent niche. All three products are air fryers / air fryer toaster ovens in Home & Kitchen — ideal for a single SEO content cluster rather than scattered pages.
  2. High-trust inventory. Ratings 4.2–4.4★ across 39.9K combined reviews; Amazon displayed 8K+ units bought in the past month across the three.
  3. Ready-made price ladder. $79.91 / $79.93 budget twins vs a $149.99 premium unit (20–33% off list at capture) enables honest "under $100 vs premium" comparison content.
  4. Promotion-driven conversion window. All three carried deal badges at capture (two "Limited time deal", one "Early Black Friday Deal") — timely hooks, but volatile (F5).
  5. Content angles map to verified specs only — dual-zone (2×3Qt), capacity/versatility (26QT, 24 functions, rotisserie), simplicity (8Qt 4-in-1, 450°F Hi-Fry) — no unverified claims required.

Open items (non-blocking, published for review)

  • F1 — supplied codes are 9-character short-link paths, not 10-character ASINs.
  • F2 — resolution hops through third-party amzlinks.in.
  • F3 — commission % unverifiable without Associates sign-in → left blank.
  • F4 — BSR unverifiable (bot-checkpoint) → left blank.
  • F5 — observed prices are volatile deal prices; re-verify before quoting.

Recommendation

Publish as-is: the site is live-ready, every asserted value has an official source link, and the five open items are documented rather than papered over. Next optimization steps: (1) obtain Associates sign-in access to fill commission data, (2) re-capture prices via Amazon-approved API/links before any price is quoted in content, (3) add click/conversion tracking once a real Associates tag is attached to the outgoing links, then run the standard campaign optimization loop (title/price re-checks, deal-expiry cleanup per Program Policies §2(b)).

Compliance & Disclosure

OA §5 implemented
As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. This is the disclosure required by the Amazon Associates Program Operating Agreement §5. It appears at the top of every page on this site (hard to miss, per FTC guidance).

1. Amazon Associates disclosure — exact requirement

Verbatim from the official Associates Program Operating Agreement (Updated: October 15, 2025), Section 5 — Identifying Yourself as an Associate:

Quoted from the Operating Agreement "You must clearly and prominently state the following, or any substantially similar statement previously allowed under this Agreement, on your Site or any other location where Amazon may authorize your display or other use of Program Content: 'As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.'"
Rule (OA §5)How this site complies
State the exact (or previously allowed substantially similar) statementExact wording used: "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." (plural "we" — substantially similar form for a site operator)OA §5
Clearly and prominently on the SiteDisclosure banner at the top of every page, above content, plus the fixed sidebar disclosure block and a footer repeatOA §5
No other public communication about the Agreement without Amazon's written permissionSite describes its own research/editorial process only; no claims of Amazon endorsement/sponsorship anywhereOA §5

Source: affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement (fetched 2026-11-13; page states "Updated: October 15, 2025"). Violation of §5 is deemed a material breach under OA §6.
